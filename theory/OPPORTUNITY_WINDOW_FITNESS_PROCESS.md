# Opportunity-window fitness process

Status: side theory on branch theory/opportunity-fitness-process-v1. This note is not yet part of the frozen manuscript. It replaces the strongest part of the ad hoc gap-to-fitness lift with an explicit natural-history process.

## 1. Biological question

The exact finite decision theory gives

\[
C_A\le C_F,
\]

where C_A is the minimum worst-path cost of contingent target resolution and C_F is the minimum cost of a precommitted resolving bundle.

The structural gap

\[
g=C_F-C_A
\]

is not intrinsically fitness. The missing biological process is whether the ecological opportunity is still available when the decision finishes.

Let B be a nonnegative random opportunity budget on the same scale as cue-acquisition cost. Examples are time before prey escapes, time before a flower or mate is lost, tolerated exposure before predation risk becomes unacceptable, or a finite energetic/handling opportunity.

Define the survival function

\[
S_B(c)=\Pr(B\ge c).
\]

Successful resolution by cost c therefore has opportunity probability S_B(c).

## 2. Theorem OF1 — exact opportunity-window mass

Suppose correct target resolution before the opportunity closes gives benefit v>=0, and compare guarantee-level completion at costs C_A<=C_F.

The expected performance benefit uniquely available to contingent resolution is

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm opp}
=
v[S_B(C_A)-S_B(C_F)]
=
v\Pr(C_A\le B<C_F).
}
\]

Proof. The contingent guarantee succeeds when B>=C_A; the fixed guarantee succeeds when B>=C_F. Since C_A<=C_F, the set difference is exactly {C_A<=B<C_F}. Multiplying its probability by v gives the result.

This removes the hard-budget discontinuity without removing the original adaptive-only window. The hard-budget model is recovered by a degenerate distribution concentrated at one value B=b.

If maintaining contingent control has additive expected-fitness debit K>=0, define

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{\rm opp}=R_{\rm opp}-K.
}
\]

Contingent control is favored in this minimal process exactly when Phi_opp>0.

This is a direct bridge to architecture-value logic: the recoverable benefit is now generated by natural-history timing rather than posited as a linear multiple of g.

## 3. Corollary — the structural gap alone is not fitness-sufficient

Two tasks can have the same

\[
g=C_F-C_A
\]

but different opportunity-window mass because

\[
\Pr(C_A\le B<C_F)
\]

depends on the location of the interval, not only its width.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
C_F-C_A
\text{ alone does not identify the fitness value of routeability.}
}
\]

The pair (C_A,C_F), together with the opportunity distribution, is biologically relevant.

If B has density f_B, then

\[
R_{\rm opp}
=
v\int_{C_A}^{C_F} f_B(b)\,db.
\]

For a small strict gap g,

\[
R_{\rm opp}
=
v f_B(C_A)g+o(g).
\]

Thus a local linear lift exists, but its coefficient is natural-history dependent:

\[
\lambda_{\rm local}=v f_B(C_A),
\]

not a universal constant.

## 4. Theorem OF2 — exponential opportunity closure gives a unique intermediate optimum

Assume opportunities close at constant hazard mu>0:

\[
B\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\mu),
\qquad
S_B(c)=e^{-\mu c}.
\]

For

\[
0<C_A<C_F,
\]

the routeability-generated opportunity benefit is

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm opp}(\mu)
=
v(e^{-\mu C_A}-e^{-\mu C_F}).
}
\]

It tends to zero as mu approaches either zero or infinity. It has one unique maximum at

\[
\boxed{
\mu^*
=
\frac{\log(C_F/C_A)}{C_F-C_A}.
}
\]

Proof. Differentiate:

\[
\frac{1}{v}\frac{dR_{\rm opp}}{d\mu}
=
-C_Ae^{-\mu C_A}
+
C_Fe^{-\mu C_F}.
\]

The stationary condition is

\[
e^{-\mu(C_F-C_A)}=C_A/C_F,
\]

which gives the stated mu*. The derivative is positive below this point and negative above it.

The maximum window mass is

\[
\boxed{
\frac{R_{\rm opp}(\mu^*)}{v}
=
\frac{C_F-C_A}{C_F}
\left(\frac{C_A}{C_F}\right)^{C_A/(C_F-C_A)}.
}
\]

Hence selection for contingent sensing is band-pass with respect to ecological closure pressure in this minimal model:

- very low hazard: both architectures usually finish;
- intermediate hazard: the saved decision time changes success probability;
- very high hazard: even the contingent architecture rarely finishes.

A constitutive cost K shifts this curve downward. Positive selection exists only if K is below the maximum opportunity benefit.

## 5. Theorem OF3 — equal structural gaps can have ordered fitness values

Write

\[
g=C_F-C_A>0.
\]

Under exponential closure,

\[
R_{\rm opp}
=
v e^{-\mu C_A}(1-e^{-\mu g}).
\]

For fixed g, v, and mu>0, this is strictly decreasing in C_A.

Therefore two decision systems with the same structural adaptive gain can experience systematically different selection:

\[
\boxed{
g_1=g_2
\not\Rightarrow
R_{{\rm opp},1}=R_{{\rm opp},2}.
}
\]

Saving one unit from a short decision can matter far more than saving one unit from a decision that is already usually too late.

This is the simplest mechanistic reason not to use

\[
s=\lambda g-\kappa
\]

as a universal fitness map.

## 6. Theorem OF4 — pathwise process and a guarantee-level lower bound

The worst-path quantities are sufficient for guaranteed resolution, but realized fitness can depend on branch-specific completion times.

Let ecological state x occur with probability p_x. Let an exact contingent policy finish in cost T_A(x), with

\[
T_A(x)\le C_A
\]

for every represented state. Let the chosen minimum fixed bundle cost C_F; because the full precommitted bundle is acquired, its cost is state-independent.

If every correctly resolved state has the same benefit v and the opportunity survival function S_B is common across states, then the realized expected performance advantage is

\[
R_{\rm path}
=
v\sum_x p_x
[S_B(T_A(x))-S_B(C_F)].
\]

Because S_B is nonincreasing and T_A(x)<=C_A,

\[
\boxed{
R_{\rm path}
\ge
v[S_B(C_A)-S_B(C_F)]
=
R_{\rm opp}.
}
\]

Thus the opportunity-window result derived only from (C_A,C_F) is a conservative lower bound on the performance benefit of a chosen worst-case-optimal contingent tree under the stated common-value/common-survival assumptions.

If state values, hazard distributions, errors, or path costs differ, the full pathwise expression is the relevant biological object:

\[
W(\pi)
=
\sum_x p_x v_x S_x(T_\pi(x))-K_\pi.
\]

This makes explicit what additional natural-history measurements are required beyond the finite guarantee theory.

## 7. Process interpretation

The causal chain is:

ecological alternatives + cue topology
-> contingent versus fixed completion costs
-> opportunity-window overlap
-> probability of completing the correct action in time
-> ecological payoff
-> fitness margin after architecture cost
-> selection

The central biological object is not routeability by itself. It is the overlap between where routeability saves decision cost and where ecological opportunities actually close.

The sharper prediction is:

Contingent sensing should be most strongly favored when the distribution of opportunity limits places substantial probability mass between the contingent and fixed completion costs.

## 8. Relation to PAYOFF

PAYOFF uses an architecture margin of the form

\[
\Phi=R-K.
\]

The present process supplies a mechanistic candidate for R:

\[
\boxed{
R
=
v\Pr(C_A\le B<C_F)
}
\]

at the guarantee level, or the pathwise expected-payoff difference when branch-specific costs and values are observed.

Thus the repositories answer different parts of one chain:

adaptive-gain:
decision topology -> opportunity-dependent recoverable benefit R

PAYOFF:
recoverable benefit R - architecture cost K -> architecture value Phi

No claim is made that all PAYOFF architecture benefits arise from information acquisition.

## 9. Prior-art boundary

Time costs, speed-accuracy tradeoffs, sequential sampling, stochastic hazards, and fitness consequences of delayed decisions are established topics. Relevant background includes Chittka et al. (2009, Trends in Ecology & Evolution), de Froment et al. (2014, PLOS Computational Biology), and Houston (2014, Journal of Animal Ecology).

The narrow repository-specific result is the composition of that natural-history timing logic with the exact adaptive/fixed guarantee pair (C_A,C_F):

\[
(C_A,C_F)
\to
\Pr(C_A\le B<C_F)
\to
R_{\rm opp}
\to
R_{\rm opp}-K.
\]

Novelty should not be claimed for exponential survival or differentiation of the resulting curve by itself.

## 10. Scope

This note still assumes exact eventual target resolution and does not yet include noisy cue observations, accuracy below full target resolution, learning costs, endogenous stopping thresholds, state-dependent opportunity values in the guarantee-level theorem, or demographic feedback after the immediate fitness map. Those belong in later process layers rather than being hidden inside C_A or C_F.
