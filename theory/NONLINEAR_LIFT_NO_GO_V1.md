# Nonlinear sensing-to-feedback no-go theorem v1

## Purpose

The flagship reachability result currently uses a linear bridge from finite sensing structure to local eco-evolutionary feedback,

\[
G=a\,\Delta g,
\qquad
\Delta g=C_F-C_A.
\]

That bridge is biologically transparent, but an upper-tier theoretical paper should not make its main ecological conclusion depend on exact linearity. This note records the stronger necessary-condition result that survives for a broad class of nonlinear sensing-to-selection maps.

The theorem is intentionally one-sided. It proves when an architecture is **too simple** to reach a requested local dynamical regime. It does not claim that every architecture above the bound will realize that regime.

---

## 1. Biological microfoundation for the linear special case

Suppose correct resolution of the declared ecological target gives the same action payoff under two sensing architectures, and each acquired cue carries an additive cost `c>0` on the declared selection/fitness scale.

Let

\[
C_A
\]

be the minimum worst-case cue-acquisition cost under adaptive outcome-contingent sensing and

\[
C_F
\]

be the minimum cost of a fixed resolving bundle. Then

\[
\Delta g=C_F-C_A\ge0
\]

is the guaranteed acquisition burden avoided by adaptive sensing.

Under additive cue costs, the corresponding worst-case structural selection advantage is

\[
\Delta s=c\,\Delta g.
\]

A state-independent maintenance debit `\kappa` gives

\[
s(g)=c g-\kappa.
\]

Thus the existing linear lift is not an arbitrary algebraic decoration: it is the minimal additive acquisition-cost model. However, the main no-go result below does not require exact additivity.

---

## 2. Nonlinear lift class

Let structural gap satisfy

\[
g\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.
\]

Let selection generated by sensing structure be

\[
s(g)=f(g)-\kappa,
\]

where `f` is nondecreasing and has bounded marginal effect on the relevant domain:

\[
0\le f(g_2)-f(g_1)\le L(g_2-g_1)
\qquad
\text{for }g_2\ge g_1,
\]

for some finite `L>0`.

The bound allows diminishing returns, saturation, piecewise-linear lifts, and other nonlinear mappings. It does not require differentiability.

Let the local ecological-to-evolutionary conversion scale be

\[
B=-\beta e>0,
\]

so that for an ordered structural contrast

\[
\Delta g=g_2-g_1\ge0,
\]

the local feedback gain is

\[
G=B\,\Delta s
=B[f(g_2)-f(g_1)].
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
0\le G\le BL\,\Delta g.
}
\]

The linear model is the special case `f(g)=\lambda g`, `L=\lambda`, and `a=B\lambda`.

---

## 3. Theorem — nonlinear architecture lower bound

For the generalized local response with evolutionary persistence `alpha` and community persistence `phi`, the complex-eigenpair boundary is

\[
G_{\rm osc}
=
\frac{(\alpha-\phi)^2}{4(1-\phi)}.
\]

A stable oscillatory response requires, in particular,

\[
G>G_{\rm osc}.
\]

Because every lift in the declared monotone-Lipschitz class obeys

\[
G\le BL\,\Delta g,
\]

a necessary condition for oscillation is

\[
BL\,\Delta g>G_{\rm osc}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Delta g>
\frac{G_{\rm osc}}{BL}.
}
\]

For integer structural gaps,

\[
\boxed{
q_{\rm osc}^{\rm necessary}
=
\left\lfloor
\frac{G_{\rm osc}}{BL}
\right\rfloor+1.
}
\]

This is exact as a necessary integer lower bound for the whole declared nonlinear class.

### Proof

Monotonicity and the Lipschitz bound give

\[
0\le\Delta s\le L\Delta g.
\]

Multiplying by `B>0` gives

\[
0\le G\le BL\Delta g.
\]

If `BL\Delta g\le G_osc`, then every admissible nonlinear lift has `G\le G_osc`, contradicting the strict requirement `G>G_osc`. Therefore `BL\Delta g>G_osc` is necessary. Integer rounding gives the stated bound. QED.

---

## 4. Corollary — architecture-insufficient regimes are impossible

Suppose a declared architecture class has a proven structural-gap ceiling

\[
\Delta g\le q_{\max}.
\]

If

\[
\boxed{
BL q_{\max}\le G_{\rm osc},
}
\]

then stable oscillatory local feedback is impossible for **every** sensing-to-selection lift in the declared monotone-Lipschitz class.

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\text{architecture too simple}
\Longrightarrow
\text{requested oscillatory regime unreachable}.
}
\]

This is the biologically useful orientation of the theorem. It turns finite sensing complexity into a falsifiable structural prohibition rather than only a statement that richer architectures can generate larger feedback.

---

## 5. Canonical binary example without exact linearity

Use the current canonical local geometry

\[
\alpha=1,
\qquad
\phi=\frac12,
\]

so

\[
G_{\rm osc}=\frac18.
\]

Declare

\[
B=\frac12,
\qquad
L=\frac14,
\]

so

\[
BL=\frac18.
\]

Then

\[
q_{\rm osc}^{\rm necessary}=2.
\]

Existing binary extremal results independently prove that all tasks with any one of the following restrictions have structural-gap ceiling at most one:

- at most 5 represented worlds;
- at most 4 declared binary queries;
- at most 4 minimal productive-frontier obligations.

Therefore all three architecture classes satisfy

\[
BL q_{\max}
=\frac18
=G_{\rm osc},
\]

and are ruled out by the nonlinear theorem.

The existing six-world, five-query, five-obligation constructive witness with gap two remains a reachability witness for the linear special case `f(g)=g/4`. Under a generic nonlinear lift above the no-go threshold, reachability is not asserted unless the realized selection contrast is separately bounded from below.

---

## 6. What this result does and does not improve

### It improves

- the main ecological conclusion no longer depends on exact linearity of `G=a Delta g`;
- the theory now supports an explicit **no-go statement** for under-complex sensing architectures;
- the linear model becomes a transparent microfounded special case rather than the only bridge;
- the reverse reachability theorem becomes robust to diminishing-return or saturating sensing-to-selection maps, provided their maximum marginal effect is bounded.

### It does not claim

- that `BL` is known empirically;
- that crossing the lower bound is sufficient for oscillation;
- that all biological sensing costs are additive;
- that `C_F-C_A` is intrinsically fitness;
- that the theorem applies to noisy probabilistic sensing without a separate extension;
- that the local linear dynamical model is a global nonlinear bifurcation theorem.

---

## 7. Publication role

For a higher-tier evolutionary-theory version of the paper, the recommended headline hierarchy is:

1. **Principal no-go/reachability theorem:** a requested local eco-evolutionary regime imposes a minimum finite decision/separation architecture; insufficient architecture makes that regime impossible.
2. **Robustness:** the lower bound survives a broad monotone-Lipschitz nonlinear sensing-to-selection class.
3. **Structural-temporal consequence:** ecological recurrence filters the structurally generated reward through community modes.
4. **Diagnostics:** oscillatory transients can establish feedback existence within the declared model class; stasis mechanisms remain distinct.

Do not promote the nonlinear extension as a separate unrelated theorem family. Its role is to close the biological bridge under the principal result.
