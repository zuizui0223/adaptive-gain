# V3 → V4 ecology-first manuscript change log

Status: narrative audit only. No theorem family or mathematical claim has been changed.

## What changed

### Headline

**V3:** finite sensing architecture defines an eco-evolutionary possibility space.

**V4:** ecological structure and deadlines govern the evolutionary value of contingent sensing.

The V4 headline is a biological prediction. Finite architecture is the mechanism underneath it.

### Introduction

V3 opened with information and finite decision architecture.

V4 opens with natural-history time pressure and incomplete information:

- predator attack;
- moving/ephemeral resources;
- host and oviposition decisions;
- flower discrimination;
- speed–accuracy trade-offs;
- mortality/exposure costs of complex resource choice.

The central question is now:

> When does the organization of information acquisition become a target of selection?

### Main ecological predictions

V4 foregrounds five predictions:

1. **Intermediate constraint:** the unique performance/selection opportunity for contingent sensing is non-monotonic across ecological constraint.
2. **Conditional cue dependence:** branch-specific relevance, not cue abundance or marginal balance, generates the largest contingent advantage.
3. **Decision-relevant heterogeneity:** environmental change matters when it changes the decision problem or budget crossing.
4. **Feedback ceiling:** small state differences cannot generate arbitrarily strong sensing-mediated eco-evolutionary feedback.
5. **Persistence × alignment:** slow ecological modes matter only when state-specific sensing selection loads onto them.

### What evolves

V4 explicitly states that the selected trait can be:

- cue order;
- stopping behavior;
- conditional attention;
- sequential motor/sampling routines;
- regulatory deployment of sensory effort.

The fixed architecture is explicitly a counterfactual non-contingent benchmark, not a claim that organisms literally sample every cue simultaneously.

### Empirical connection

V4 adds an explicit experimental program:

- observe conditional cue sequences;
- reconstruct the focal decision tree;
- build a fixed counterfactual;
- manipulate time/exposure/opportunity budget;
- compare ecological states;
- estimate state recurrence and selection alignment.

### Natural-history systems

The manuscript now uses three illustrative mappings:

- predator assessment;
- herbivore host/oviposition choice;
- pollinator flower choice.

They are clearly labeled as hypotheses/mappings rather than fitted demonstrations.

### Ecological literature added

The framing now directly uses work on:

- predation-risk decision making;
- speed–accuracy trade-offs;
- information-processing limits and specialization;
- host-search accuracy in complex environments;
- proximate decision architecture.
