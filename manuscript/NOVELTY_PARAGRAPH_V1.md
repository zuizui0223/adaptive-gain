# Novelty paragraph v4

Evolutionary ecology already treats information acquisition, sampling, memory, and information-processing limits as factors shaping adaptive decisions and evolutionary responses. Information-theoretic work also already quantifies the fitness value of environmental information and, importantly, asks how much mutual information is minimally required to achieve a target population growth rate or average selection coefficient (Donaldson-Matasci et al. 2010; Rivoire & Leibler 2011; Moffett & Eckford 2022). Work on information thresholds likewise links evolutionary dynamics to limits on maintainable or accumulable coding information (de Boer & Hogeweg 2010). Computer science and test theory separately characterize fixed separating systems, adaptive decision trees, multiway tests, and adaptivity gaps, while fluctuating-selection and eco-evolutionary theory already explain how rapid short-term change can fail to accumulate or can be dynamically restored.

We therefore do **not** claim novelty for information use, the fitness value of information, minimum mutual-information requirements for fitness or selection, generic information thresholds, cognitive constraints, sequential sampling, bounded-outcome decision trees, the bounded-arity tree extremal formulas used internally, adaptivity, rapid evolution, stasis, or feedback themselves. Our contribution lies in a narrower ecological composition. The paper uses **finite deterministic decision/separation structure**, rather than Shannon information, as its structural coordinate: represented alternatives `n`, declared cues `m`, irreducible fixed-side obligations `E`, adaptive depth, and outcome arity. Within that declared model class, finite structure places exact discrete ceilings on structurally generated selection, recurrence of the same community states filters those rewards through time, and a required local feedback regime implies a minimum or Pareto-minimal **finite decision/separation structure**. Thus the finite combinatorial architecture of individual information use restricts which evolutionary fluctuations and local feedback phases are structurally reachable.
