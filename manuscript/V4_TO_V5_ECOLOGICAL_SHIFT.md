# V4 → V5 ecological shift

Status: narrative audit. V4 remains machine-frozen and unchanged as the submission fallback. V5 is a separate environment-centered candidate.

## Central change

### V4

Primary question:

> When does the organization of information acquisition become a target of selection?

Primary subject:

- contingent sensing;
- cue order;
- stopping behavior;
- organismal information acquisition.

### V5

Primary question:

> When does ecological diversity translate into decision-relevant complexity, and when can hierarchical environmental structure prevent that translation?

Primary subject:

- environmental heterogeneity;
- ecological diversity;
- decision-equivalence classes;
- niche breadth;
- potential versus behaviorally accessible interaction networks;
- temporal environmental structure.

The mathematics is unchanged. The interpretation is reversed from organism → environment.

## Main ecological conclusions promoted in V5

### 1. Diversity is not complexity

Resource richness, species richness or environmental state richness does not determine how many distinctions must remain jointly unresolved in a focal ecological interaction.

The relevant property is **environmental routeability**.

### 2. Richness and interaction-relevant richness can diverge

Target-relevant state reduction is reinterpreted as an ecological equivalence result.

Adding species or states inside an existing decision-equivalence class can increase raw richness without increasing contingent resolution cost.

### 3. Routeability modifies the information cost of generalism

The sequential-cue structure is no longer presented mainly as a behavioral strategy.

It becomes an environmental condition under which broad resource use can remain compatible with finite information budgets.

Claim ceiling: routeability does not determine niche breadth by itself.

### 4. Routeability constrains accessible interaction networks

Potential interactions allowed by encounter, morphology and energetics need not all be behaviorally accessible under finite information budgets.

Routeability can enlarge the accessible subset.

Claim ceiling: accessible interactions are not automatically realized interactions.

### 5. Temporal heterogeneity is filtered by decision relevance

Environmental autocorrelation alone is insufficient.

Persistence matters when recurrent states differ in routeability or other decision-relevant structure.
