## Letter format

- Article type: **Letter**.
- Typical length: **approximately 5,000 words or less**, excluding display items.
- Title: **30 words or less**.
- Abstract: **300 words maximum**.
- Keywords: **10 maximum**.
- Required main structure: Introduction; Methods; Results; Discussion; References. Results and Discussion may be merged if observations and interpretations remain distinguishable.
- Teaser text is encouraged and should be **150 words maximum**, before the Abstract.
- The journal explicitly welcomes **theoretical studies** and analytical or methodological frameworks with broad potential influence.
- End matter before References includes Data and code availability, Author contributions, Funding, Conflict of interest, and Acknowledgements if applicable.
- Submission is through ScholarOne.
