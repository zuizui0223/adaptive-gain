Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Finite sensing architecture excludes eco-evolutionary feedback regimes,”** for publication as a Letter in *Evolution Letters*.

The paper asks a reverse evolutionary question: rather than choosing a sensing architecture and asking what dynamics it produces, what finite sensing architecture is minimally required before a specified eco-evolutionary feedback regime is even possible?

For a declared finite ecological decision problem, we distinguish the minimum worst-case cue cost of adaptive outcome-contingent sensing from the minimum fixed cue bundle that guarantees the same resolution. Their difference defines a structural gap. We show that a requested local feedback regime imposes a lower bound on this gap and therefore an exact binary minimum, or under bounded cue arity a Pareto-minimal requirement, on represented ecological alternatives, cue resources and irreducible separation obligations.

The main biological conclusion does not depend on exact proportionality between sensing structure and fitness. For any nondecreasing sensing-to-selection map with bounded marginal effect, insufficient structural complexity makes the requested oscillatory feedback regime impossible. Thus the paper yields a no-go statement: some finite sensing architectures exclude entire classes of local eco-evolutionary dynamics. Ecological recurrence then determines which structurally generated selection contrasts persist through time.

We position the result conservatively relative to established theory. We do not claim novelty for the fitness value of information, minimum mutual-information requirements, decision trees, separating systems, generic adaptivity gaps, fluctuating selection, or eco-evolutionary feedback. The contribution is their discrete evolutionary composition into a reverse architecture-to-reachability theorem and a nonlinear structural impossibility result.

We believe the manuscript fits *Evolution Letters* because it is a concise theoretical study with a single broad evolutionary claim, connects information-use architecture to evolutionary feedback, and provides exact limits on which regimes are structurally reachable. The main text is designed to remain within the journal's Letter-length guidance, with technical combinatorial proofs and exhaustive finite validation kept outside the main narrative.

All code used to verify the theoretical results is publicly available in the `zuizui0223/adaptive-gain` repository. A permanent archival identifier will be supplied at submission if available.
