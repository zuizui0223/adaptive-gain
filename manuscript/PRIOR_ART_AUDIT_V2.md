# Prior-art audit v2 — narrowed boundary after final search

## Purpose

This note records the conservative novelty boundary after targeted searches in evolutionary ecology, information theory, test cover, separating systems, decision-tree theory, and eco-evolutionary dynamics. It separates the manuscript's finite combinatorial coordinate from established Shannon-information and fitness-value theory.

## 1. Sequential and adaptive information use is established prior art

Evolutionary ecology already treats information acquisition and use as adaptive decision making, including repeated sampling, state dependence, and ecological consequences. Dall et al. (2005), Schmidt et al. (2010), Dukas (2004), Trimmer & Houston (2014), Eliassen et al. (2016), and Wright et al. (2022) establish this broad boundary.

Therefore the manuscript must not claim novelty for sequential cue use, adaptive information acquisition, cue ordering, state-dependent information use, information-processing architecture, or feedback between action and subsequently available information.

## 2. Fitness value of information and minimum informational requirements are established prior art

A closer literature formalizes the relationship between environmental information and evolutionary fitness.

- Donaldson-Matasci, Bergstrom & Lachmann (2010) formalize the fitness value of environmental information and relate cue information to long-run growth.
- Rivoire & Leibler (2011) develop information-fitness relations for populations in varying environments.
- Moffett & Eckford (2022) use rate-distortion theory to ask explicitly how much information is minimally required for a population to achieve a target growth rate and how much information gain is minimally required for one subpopulation to achieve a target average selection coefficient over another.
- de Boer & Hogeweg (2010) analyze an evolutionary information threshold for coding structure in eco-evolutionary dynamics.

This materially narrows the manuscript's language. It must **not** claim novelty for `minimum information required for fitness`, `minimum information required for selection`, the fitness value of information, information-fitness tradeoffs, or generic evolutionary information thresholds.

The present coordinate is different: it is a finite deterministic decision/separation architecture described by represented alternatives, declared cues, adaptive depth, fixed separation obligations, and outcome arity. Its principal result is an exact discrete/Pareto constraint on that structure, not a rate-distortion or mutual-information bound.

## 3. Fixed separation and adaptive decision trees are established prior art

The fixed identification problem overlaps classical separating systems / minimum test set / test cover. The adaptive problem overlaps optimal decision trees and adaptive search. Multiway decision trees are also established; Chakaravarthy et al. (2009) explicitly study decision trees with multiway branches.

Therefore the manuscript must not claim novelty for binary or multiway decision trees, bounded numbers of outcomes per test as a modelling idea, minimum separating systems, fixed-vs-adaptive cost comparisons, or generic adaptivity gaps.

## 4. Bounded test size is not bounded query arity

Classical separating-system / Test Cover work by Katona, Wegener, Crowston and others constrains the cardinality of a test set. The repository parameter `b` instead constrains the number of outcomes/parts generated by one query. These are neighboring but different restrictions.

Do not cite bounded-test-size results as equivalence proofs for bounded outcome arity. Multiway decision-tree literature is the relevant conceptual prior art.

## 5. The bounded-arity extremal recurrence is not a manuscript novelty claim

The repository quantity `F_b(n,h)` is the maximum number of internal-node occurrences in a productive rooted decision tree with bounded leaf count, depth, and out-degree. Its recurrence is elementary rooted-tree decomposition, and the downstream sharp constructions use standard decision-tree / separating-test ingredients.

No priority claim should depend on whether an identical `F_b` formula appears in print. The manuscript treats the recurrence, endpoint formulas, private-pair witness construction, and bounded-arity extremal counts as supporting combinatorial machinery.

## 6. Productive frontier is repository terminology, not a classical-priority claim

The audit found standard language for minimal / irredundant test covers and separating systems, but no exact published synonym for the repository's productive frontier. This does not establish novelty.

Use the cautious formulation:

- `productive frontier` is repository terminology for the irreducible separation obligations retained by the declared fixed-cost problem;
- edge-count consequences are consequences of that construction;
- do not claim a new classical invariant.

## 7. Shared-state-space reward and recurrence

Markov-switching environments, fluctuating selection, temporal autocorrelation, and eco-evolutionary feedback are all established. The candidate composition is that the same finite community-state space both carries structurally generated rewards `g_i` and supplies the transition operator determining temporal recurrence, allowing a structural-temporal extremal envelope.

This should not be advertised as a new theory of temporal autocorrelation or information-fitness value. The paper's supporting result is the particular composition of a finite decision/separation reward range with reversible recurrence, including the reward-mode alignment/tightness decomposition.

## 8. Exact novelty boundary after the final search

The strongest defensible statement is:

> Existing theory already covers evolutionary information use and information-processing constraints, the fitness value of information, minimal mutual-information requirements for target fitness or selection, evolutionary information thresholds, separating systems, binary and multiway decision trees, adaptivity gaps, fluctuating selection, and eco-evolutionary feedback. We also make no novelty claim for the bounded-arity tree extremal formulas or standard dynamical-systems ingredients used internally. The present contribution is narrower: finite deterministic decision/separation structure is composed with state-indexed selection and local feedback so that a required local dynamical regime implies an exact minimum or Pareto-minimal finite structural requirement over represented alternatives, declared cues, and irreducible obligations. Community recurrence then supplies a supporting extremal envelope on the same structurally generated rewards.

The most important distinction from Moffett & Eckford (2022) is therefore not `minimum information` versus `no minimum information`. Both theories contain a reverse requirement. The distinction is the **object being minimized and the downstream target**:

- Moffett & Eckford: Shannon/mutual-information rate required for target growth or average selection coefficient;
- present manuscript: finite deterministic decision/separation architecture required for a declared adaptive/fixed structural gap and, after the ecological lift, for a local feedback phase; bounded arity generally yields a Pareto set over `(n,m,E)` rather than a scalar bit rate.

## 9. Final priority-audit status

The targeted pre-submission search was completed on 2026-09-10. It found a substantially closer reverse-direction precursor than the earlier audit had recorded: Moffett & Eckford (2022). This does **not** invalidate the principal theorem, because the theorem concerns a different structural object, but it invalidates any broad novelty wording that could be read as claiming the first minimum-information requirement for fitness, selection, or evolutionary dynamics.

Accordingly:

- use `minimum finite decision/separation structure` or explicit `(n,m,E)` language for the principal theorem;
- avoid unqualified `minimum information` as a novelty phrase;
- cite the information-fitness literature in the Introduction and Discussion;
- retain `our contribution is the exact discrete ecological composition` rather than priority language;
- do not use `first`, `first-ever`, `no previous theory`, or analogous claims.
