# Figure legends — relational routeability v1

## Figure 1. Entropic equivalence does not imply adaptive equivalence

**A**, One physical 12-state, four-cue environment with two alternative focal
target/action maps. Every binary cue is exactly 6:6 balanced and the cue matrix
is identical between the two tasks. **B**, The two tasks have the same complete
Shannon entropy vector: \(H_A(U)=H_B(U)\) for every subset of the target and
four cues. Consequently their fixed exact-resolution costs are identical,
\(C_F=4\), because fixed resolution is characterized by
\(H(T\mid Q_S)=0\). **C**, Optimal adaptive resolution differs despite this
static entropic equivalence: \(C_A=4\) for Twin A and (C_A=3) for Twin B.
The difference arises because realized cue outcomes leave different
continuation problems. **D**, The distinction is not confined to a small
finite example. Under \(r\)-fold Cartesian products, complete Shannon entropic
equivalence is preserved while
\((C_A,C_F)=(4r,4r)\) for Twin A and
\((3r,4r)\) for Twin B, so the adaptive-cost difference grows as \(r\).

## Figure 2. Finite budgets connect information accessibility to ecology

**A**, Routeability changes guaranteed feasibility only in the intermediate
budget window \(C_A\le B<C_F\); below \(C_A\) neither access mode is sufficient,
whereas above \(C_F\) both are sufficient. **B**, *Bombus* as a single-system empirical anchor. Independent public
datasets from the same genus instantiate complementary operational ingredients
of the theory. In the bioRxiv preprint by Yuan et al. (2026),
platform landing increases with decision difficulty when landing is required to
obtain a predictive cue (Regular trials), but the gradient reverses when the cue
is supplied independently of landing (Random Free-Cue trials). The within-bee
Impossible-versus-Easy difference-in-differences is 1.018 and positive for
191/192 bees. In Spaethe et al. (2026), a prospectively defined secondary-cue
recruitment index rises from \(R=0.026\) when the primary colour cue is easy to
\(R=0.374\) when it is difficult (pooled Cliff's
\(\delta=0.980\)). MaBouDi et al. (2025), Chow et al. (2022), and Essenberg et
al. (2015) provide complementary evidence for selective sequential sampling,
context-conditioned action mapping, and branch-specific cue relevance,
respectively. These datasets validate operational components of routeability;
none alone estimates the exact deterministic \(C_A<C_F\) contrast. **C**, The
prospective causal test holds one physical cue matrix fixed, changes only the
action-conditioned branch structure, manipulates costly cue access under a
shared budget, and predicts no architecture-by-access effect below or above the
routeability-sensitive window but a positive effect inside it. **D**, Empirical
measurement begins with a focal action, represented alternatives, cue outcomes
and cue costs; \(C_A\) and \(C_F\) are computed from that action-conditioned
table before comparison with the biological budget.
