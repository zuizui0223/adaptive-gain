Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Finite sensing architecture defines an eco-evolutionary possibility space,”** for publication as a Letter in *Evolution Letters*.

The paper asks how the finite architecture of contingent information use constrains not only decision efficiency but which ecological performances, selective regimes and local eco-evolutionary feedbacks are possible. For a declared finite decision target, we compare the minimum worst-case cost of an adaptive decision tree with the minimum fixed cue bundle that guarantees the same resolution. This exact finite geometry supplies a common mathematical backbone for several biological consequences.

First, adaptive sensing alone can meet a hard ecological time, energy or opportunity budget exactly when that budget lies between the adaptive and fixed resolution costs. Under a threshold fitness model, this becomes an exact selection window for contingent sensing without assuming that every saved unit of cue acquisition maps linearly to fitness. Second, sharp extremal results translate a required adaptive-versus-fixed structural gap into exact binary or Pareto-minimal requirements on represented alternatives, cue resources and irreducible fixed-side obligations. The advantage is not explained by simple cue prevalence: we construct exactly 50/50 balanced binary query families in which the fixed/adaptive cost ratio is unbounded.

Third, the paper connects this finite structure to eco-evolutionary dynamics. Across ecological states, differences in the adaptive-versus-fixed structural gap bound the available state-dependent selection contrast. For any nondecreasing sensing-to-selection map with bounded marginal effect, insufficient structural contrast makes a requested oscillatory feedback regime impossible. This is a no-go result: crossing the bound is necessary but does not generically guarantee oscillation. Ecological recurrence then determines which structurally generated selection contrasts persist through time, depending on their alignment with slow ecological modes.

The contribution is therefore not a new generic information-fitness principle and not a lower bound on Shannon information. It is a finite architectural theory linking natural-history alternatives and cue resources to exact decision cost, ecological budget exposure, state-dependent selection, dynamical reachability and temporal filtering. Target-relevant quotient results additionally clarify which descriptive differences in worlds or cues can be removed without changing the exact contingent decision problem.

We believe this synthesis fits *Evolution Letters* because the mathematics delivers one broad evolutionary message: finite decision architecture defines a constrained eco-evolutionary possibility space. The main text retains only mathematics that changes the biological interpretation; exact Bellman-kernel proofs, finite constructions and certificate machinery remain in the Supplement and repository.

All code used to verify the theoretical results is publicly available in the `zuizui0223/adaptive-gain` repository. A permanent archival identifier will be supplied at submission if available.
