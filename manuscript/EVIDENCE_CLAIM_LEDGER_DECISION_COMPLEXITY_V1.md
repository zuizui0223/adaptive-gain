# Evidence-to-claim ledger — decision complexity framing v1

Status: submission framing control for the integrated routeability manuscript.

## Positioning

Primary field: behavioural ecology / decision ecology.

Secondary fields: cognitive ecology, theoretical ecology, information ecology.

Mathematical machinery: decision-tree optimization, information theory and
finite exact-resolution theory.

Primary ecological question:

> When does ecological diversity actually become decision complexity?

## Evidence and permitted claims

### E1. Broad choice sets can impose information-processing costs

Evidence:
- Bernays & Funk (1999): specialist aphids made host-associated decisions
  faster than generalists.
- Bernays & Wcislo (1994) and Bernays (2001): information-processing and neural
  constraints as mechanisms linking resource specialization to choice costs.

Permitted claim:
- Information-processing cost is an established candidate constraint on broad
  resource use.

Not permitted:
- Broad niche breadth universally causes high cognitive cost.

### E2. Natural ecological decisions are often sequential or hierarchical

Evidence:
- Stephens (2008): foraging has sequential foreground-background structure and
  a hierarchy from habitat choice to food choice.
- Silva & Clarke (2020): polyphagous herbivores can use general and specific
  host cues sequentially.
- Quilici & Rousse (2012): parasitoids locate host habitat before host.
- Hemmi & Pfeil (2010): fiddler crabs acquire qualitatively different risk
  information during successive escape stages.

Permitted claim:
- Staged information acquisition occurs across distinct ecological decision
  contexts and taxa.

Not permitted:
- These systems have measured routeability or establish \(C_A<C_F\).

### E3. Bumblebees are a model flexible generalist system

Evidence:
- Gegear & Laverty (2005): flower constancy and foraging rate respond to
  multi-trait variability rather than simply the number of colours.
- Russell et al. (2017): generalist *Bombus impatiens* switches between two
  cue-regulated pollen-collection routines across diverse floral resources.
- Spaethe et al. (2026): *B. terrestris* recruits secondary visual cues when
  primary colour information is difficult.
- Essenberg et al. (2015), Lotto & Chittka (2005), Dale et al. (2005), and
  MaBouDi et al. (2025): context-conditioned cue relevance/action and
  sequential sensory sampling.

Permitted claim:
- *Bombus* demonstrates the biological operations that make conditional
  decision structure relevant in a flexible generalist.

Not permitted:
- Bumblebees have been shown to optimize \(C_A\), or natural bumblebee
  routeability has been measured.

### E4. Exact theoretical result

Evidence:
- 12-state matched twins on one identical physical cue matrix.
- Complete Shannon entropy vector matches exactly.
- \((C_A,C_F)_A=(4,4)\), \((C_A,C_F)_B=(3,4)\).
- Independent implementation and exact fingerprints verify the construction.

Permitted claim:
- Static Shannon information can determine fixed exact-resolution burden while
  failing to determine adaptive worst-case burden.
- Resource count, cue count and static information are not sufficient in
  principle to identify effective sequential decision complexity.

Not permitted:
- Shannon information is generally ecologically uninformative.

### E5. Ecological budget consequence

Evidence:
- Exact corollary \(C_A\le B<C_F\).

Permitted claim:
- Under a justified hard observation budget, conditional structure can change
  guaranteed behavioural feasibility.

Not permitted:
- Natural systems generally occupy this budget window.

### E6. Evolutionary consequence

Evidence:
- Monotone-Lipschitz bound \(G\le B_fL\Delta g\).

Permitted claim:
- Within the declared local model class, insufficient structural contrast can
  rule out a specified feedback regime.

Role:
- Secondary consequence, not the ecological motivation or headline novelty.

## General principle

The manuscript supports the following general statement:

> Ecological diversity becomes decision complexity only to the extent that its
> alternatives and cues must be distinguished together before action.

The manuscript does not yet establish the prevalence or distribution of
routeability in natural communities. That is the empirical program generated
by the theory.
