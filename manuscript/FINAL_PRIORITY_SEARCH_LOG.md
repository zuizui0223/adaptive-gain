# Final targeted priority-search log

## Purpose

This file records the final targeted novelty search used to discipline the manuscript claim. It is not evidence that no predecessor exists. It records what nearby literatures were explicitly checked and why they do or do not collapse the candidate contribution.

## Search target

The only priority question that matters for the manuscript is now:

> Is there an ecological/evolutionary theory that maps finite cue/decision complexity into an exact ceiling on long-run evolutionary fluctuation or into a minimum information complexity required to reach a local eco-evolutionary feedback phase?

The manuscript makes no priority claim for the component mathematics.

## Nearby ecology/evolution literatures checked

### Information use and cognitive constraints

- Dall et al. (2005): information use in evolutionary ecology and statistical decision theory.
- Schmidt et al. (2010): ecology of information and informed decisions.
- Dukas (2004): limited attention / information-processing rate as an evolutionary-ecological constraint with fitness consequences.
- complex-cue / plasticity literature: multiple cues can improve reliability and plastic responses.
- Wright (2022): sampling effort, memory, cue reliability, temporal autocorrelation, and plasticity jointly evolve; sampling cost constrains plasticity.

Conclusion: information constraints limiting adaptive/evolutionary response are prior art.

### Eco-evolutionary feedback

- Post & Palkovacs (2009), Schoener (2011): generic reciprocal eco-evolutionary feedback is prior art.
- Croll, Egas & Smallegange (2019): explicit population-dynamic / trait-expression eco-evolutionary feedback in alternative reproductive tactics.
- many later eco-evolutionary models close loops between ecological state, selection, and trait dynamics.

Conclusion: endogenous feedback, feedback-modified selection, and trait/environment recurrence are prior art.

### Fluctuating selection and temporal recurrence

- Bell (2010), Estes & Arnold (2007), Uyeda et al. (2011), Messer et al. (2016), Cotto & Chevin (2020): rapid evolution, stasis, fluctuating selection, temporal autocorrelation, and scale-dependent evolutionary change are prior art.

Conclusion: short-term activity versus long-term retention is not itself novel.

## Nearby combinatorial / decision literatures checked

- minimum test set / test cover / separating systems;
- binary and multiway optimal decision trees;
- adaptive search and adaptivity gaps;
- bounded test-size separating systems;
- finite/infinite information-system decision-tree complexity;
- many-valued decision tables.

Conclusion: no manuscript novelty is assigned to these components. In particular, bounded test size and bounded query outcome arity are distinct restrictions, and multiway decision-tree theory is the appropriate conceptual prior art for the latter.

## Direct-predecessor result of the targeted search

The targeted searches did not identify a paper with the full chain

`finite decision/separation complexity`
`-> exact structural ceiling on state-dependent selection / feedback gain`
`-> recurrence of those same state-indexed rewards`
`-> sharp ceiling on long-run evolutionary fluctuation or exact minimum/Pareto-minimal information structure required for a feedback phase`.

Several papers contain one or two pieces of this chain, but none located in this search supplied the full exact composition.

This is not a proof of priority. The submission should therefore use:

- `our contribution is the exact ecological composition ...`
- `we derive ... within the declared model class`

and should avoid:

- `for the first time`;
- `the first theory`;
- `no previous work`.

## Submission-safe novelty statement

> Existing theory separately establishes evolutionary information use and cognitive constraints, fixed and adaptive identification complexity, fluctuating selection, temporal autocorrelation, and eco-evolutionary feedback. Here these components are composed into a finite-information reachability theory: declared sensing complexity bounds structurally generated selection, recurrence of the same community states filters those rewards through time, and required dynamical regimes imply minimum or Pareto-minimal finite information structures. The contribution is this exact ecological reachability composition, not the component decision, combinatorial, or dynamical machinery.

## Status

For a Theoretical Ecology submission this priority audit is sufficient to write a conservative Introduction and Discussion. A final pre-submission database search should still be performed, but no unresolved mathematical-priority question needs to block manuscript drafting.
