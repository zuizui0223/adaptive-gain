# Final targeted priority-search log

## Purpose

This file records the final targeted novelty search used to discipline the manuscript claim. It is not evidence that no predecessor exists. It records which nearby literatures were explicitly checked and how the manuscript's claim was narrowed in response.

## Search target

The priority question was refined during the final pass. A broad question such as

> Has anyone derived a minimum information requirement for fitness or selection?

is already answered **yes** by established information-fitness theory, most directly Moffett & Eckford (2022). The remaining manuscript-specific question is narrower:

> Has ecological/evolutionary theory mapped a required local feedback regime into an exact lower bound or Pareto frontier on a finite deterministic decision/separation architecture — represented alternatives, declared cues, and irreducible fixed-side obligations — through an adaptive/fixed structural gap?

The manuscript makes no priority claim for the component mathematics or for minimum informational requirements in the Shannon/rate-distortion sense.

## Nearby ecology/evolution literatures checked

### Information use, cognition, and sensing architecture

- Dall et al. (2005): information use in evolutionary ecology and statistical decision theory.
- Schmidt et al. (2010): ecology of information and informed decisions.
- Dukas (2004): limited attention / information-processing rate as an evolutionary-ecological constraint with fitness consequences.
- Trimmer & Houston (2014): evolutionary shaping of information-processing and decision mechanisms.
- Eliassen et al. (2016): proximate architecture from sensing and information processing to behavioural control embedded in evolutionary modelling.
- Wright et al. (2022): sampling effort, memory, cue reliability, temporal autocorrelation, and plasticity.

Conclusion: information constraints and sensing architecture limiting adaptive/evolutionary response are prior art.

### Fitness value of information and reverse minimum-information requirements

- Donaldson-Matasci, Bergstrom & Lachmann (2010): formal fitness value of environmental information and long-run population growth.
- Rivoire & Leibler (2011): information-fitness relations in varying environments.
- Moffett & Eckford (2022): rate-distortion formulation of the minimal information needed to attain a specified growth rate and the minimal information gain needed to attain a specified average selection coefficient.
- de Boer & Hogeweg (2010): eco-evolutionary dynamics with an evolvable coding structure and an information threshold.

Conclusion: the broad reverse idea `required fitness/selection -> required information` is **prior art**. The manuscript must not claim otherwise. Its distinction must be the discrete finite decision/separation object and its exact connection to a local feedback phase and `(n,m,E)` Pareto structure.

### Eco-evolutionary feedback

- Post & Palkovacs (2009), Schoener (2011): generic reciprocal eco-evolutionary feedback is prior art.
- many later eco-evolutionary models close loops between ecological state, selection, and trait dynamics.

Conclusion: endogenous feedback, feedback-modified selection, oscillation, and trait/environment recurrence are prior art.

### Fluctuating selection and temporal recurrence

- Bell (2010), Estes & Arnold (2007), Uyeda et al. (2011), Messer et al. (2016), Cotto & Chevin (2020): rapid evolution, stasis, fluctuating selection, temporal autocorrelation, and scale-dependent evolutionary change are prior art.

Conclusion: short-term activity versus long-term retention is not itself novel.

## Nearby combinatorial / decision literatures checked

- minimum test set / test cover / separating systems;
- binary and multiway optimal decision trees;
- adaptive search and adaptivity gaps;
- bounded test-size separating systems;
- finite/infinite information-system decision-tree complexity;
- many-valued decision tables.

Conclusion: no manuscript novelty is assigned to these components. In particular, bounded test size and bounded query outcome arity are distinct restrictions, and multiway decision-tree theory is the appropriate conceptual prior art for the latter.

## Final pre-submission pass (2026-09-10)

The final search combined ecological/evolutionary terms (`information processing`, `sensing architecture`, `fitness value of information`, `selection coefficient`, `eco-evolutionary feedback`, `information threshold`, `evolutionary dynamics`) with structural/reverse terms (`finite information`, `minimum information`, `decision tree`, `information complexity`, `rate distortion`, `required information`).

The most consequential additional result was Moffett & Eckford (2022), *Minimal informational requirements for fitness*. Its abstract explicitly asks the minimal amount of information needed for a population to achieve a target growth rate and the minimal information gain needed for one subpopulation to achieve a target average selection coefficient. This is a genuine reverse-direction precursor and rules out any broad novelty framing based on `minimum information required for fitness/selection`.

Donaldson-Matasci et al. (2010) and Rivoire & Leibler (2011) establish the broader information-fitness framework; de Boer & Hogeweg (2010) shows that `information threshold` also has an established evolutionary meaning. Trimmer & Houston (2014) and Eliassen et al. (2016) remain close precursors for evolving information-processing/sensing architecture.

## Direct-predecessor result of the targeted search

After narrowing the object, the search did not identify a paper with the full chain

`finite deterministic decision/separation architecture`
`-> adaptive/fixed structural gap`
`-> state-indexed selection or local feedback gain`
`-> required local feedback phase`
`-> exact minimum/Pareto-minimal structure over (n,m,E)`.

The search also did not identify a predecessor composing that finite structural reward object with the same-state reversible recurrence envelope used in the supporting theorem.

Several papers contain substantial neighboring pieces, especially Moffett & Eckford's reverse information-fitness requirement. Therefore non-discovery is not proof of priority and should not be described as such.

## Submission-safe novelty statement

> Existing theory already quantifies the fitness value of environmental information and can derive minimal mutual-information requirements for target growth or selection. Evolutionary ecology also treats information-processing constraints and sensing architectures as evolving mechanisms, while test theory and computer science characterize separating systems and adaptive/multiway decision trees, and eco-evolutionary theory covers temporal recurrence and feedback. The present contribution is narrower: using finite deterministic decision/separation structure as the coordinate, a required local feedback regime is mapped through an adaptive/fixed structural gap to exact lower bounds or a Pareto frontier over represented alternatives, declared cues, and irreducible obligations; recurrence of the same ecological states supplies a supporting extremal fluctuation envelope.

Use:

- `our contribution is the exact discrete ecological composition ...`;
- `minimum finite decision/separation structure`;
- `minimum/Pareto-minimal (n,m,E) structure`;
- `within the declared model class`.

Avoid:

- unqualified `minimum information` as a novelty phrase;
- `for the first time`;
- `the first theory`;
- `no previous work`.

## Status

The final targeted pre-submission search was completed on 2026-09-10 and materially narrowed the novelty wording. Moffett & Eckford (2022) is now treated as the closest reverse-direction mathematical precursor. No unresolved priority issue blocks the manuscript so long as the submitted text distinguishes Shannon/mutual-information requirements from the finite deterministic decision/separation architecture used here and avoids categorical priority claims.
