# Routing second paper — journal fit v1

Date: 2026-09-13

## Current manuscript identity

This assessment applies to the **mode + majority** version of the routing paper:

- finite branch-product genotype-policy representation;
- exact routing-layer multiplicities;
- nested-chain global bound;
- exact aggregate-mode threshold;
- exact stationary-majority threshold/bracket;
- Moran population-size corollaries;
- representation and neutral-measure claim ceilings;
- no empirical biological qualification of the branch-product representation yet.

It does not assess the frozen Theoretical Ecology flagship.

## Ranking

### 1. Theoretical Population Biology — best current fit

**Recommendation: primary target.**

Current scope explicitly centers mathematical theory and models for population biology, particularly evolution and genetics, with emphasis on substantial mathematical effort that produces biological insight.

This paper fits that identity directly:

- weak-mutation/origin-fixation population genetics is the bridge;
- the question is stationary population occupancy under a genotype–phenotype representation;
- the contribution is a finite population-genetic representation theorem rather than a purely combinatorial exercise;
- recent TPB papers continue to publish exact theoretical population-genetic results, including mutation-rate modifier theory and exact coalescent calculations.

### Main editorial risk

The isolated mathematics is elementary. The submission therefore must foreground the biological/population-genetic consequence of the **composition**, especially the distinction between:

- the fittest individual genotype;
- the largest aggregate gain class;
- a stationary majority.

The representation and mutation-measure scope controls help because they turn the theorem into a statement about what genotype–phenotype structure contributes to population-genetic prediction.

### Required pre-submission state

- exact-object prior-art audit remains clean;
- compact 3-figure story;
- manuscript does not read like repository documentation;
- population-genetic significance appears before proof detail.

---

### 2. Journal of Theoretical Biology — plausible but higher biological-significance risk

JTB explicitly welcomes theoretical biology across evolution and mathematical/computational modeling, but its current scope also states that acceptable papers must bear significant importance on the biology itself and that papers consisting only of technical mathematics without new biological insight are not considered.

The present paper is not mathematics-only, but its genotype-policy representation is still formal rather than empirically qualified. Therefore JTB is plausible **only if** the Introduction and Discussion make a strong biological case for why explicit representation-induced multiplicity is a general modeling issue in evolvable sensing/routing systems.

### Upgrade condition

JTB becomes more attractive if one empirical system independently qualifies the branch-product or a closely related heritable modular representation. Do not retrofit such a case by analogy.

---

### 3. Bulletin of Mathematical Biology — viable mathematical-biology fallback

BMB publishes structured mathematical biology and currently includes exact analyses of discretely structured biological systems. The routing paper is within broad scope.

The risk is the opposite of JTB: the core proof is short and elementary relative to some BMB work. The paper would need to emphasize the general modeling lesson and exact scope controls rather than present `A_s<=(2^k-1)^s` as mathematically deep.

BMB is a credible fallback if TPB judges the representation too specialized for population biology.

---

### 4. Evolution — stretch target in the current no-data form

Evolution explicitly welcomes significant theoretical investigations across evolutionary biology. The subject is not out of scope.

However, the current result is conditional on a deliberately chosen genotype-policy representation and symmetric neutral measure. Without empirical representation qualification or a broader theorem class, the likely editorial question is whether the result changes evolutionary understanding broadly enough for the journal.

**Current recommendation: do not lead with Evolution.**

Reconsider only if biological qualification supplies a real system in which the representation and mutation coordinates are independently defended.

---

### 5. Journal of Mathematical Biology — scope-compatible but not the best mathematical match

JMB is explicitly a venue for advanced mathematical methods applied to biological problems. The current routing result is rigorous but its decisive mathematics is intentionally elementary and finite-combinatorial.

That makes JMB less natural than TPB or BMB unless the manuscript is substantially generalized mathematically. Such generalization is **not** recommended merely to chase the journal.

## Not recommended as current first targets

### General ecology journals

The paper currently lacks an empirically qualified ecological/genetic routing representation. A general ecology pitch would therefore overstate biological reach relative to the theorem actually proved.

### Genomics-focused journals

The paper does not analyze genome-scale data or a genome-specific mechanism. A formal genotype-policy representation is not enough to make the work genomics.

## Submission strategy

Current sequence:

1. **Theoretical Population Biology**
2. **Journal of Theoretical Biology** if biological interpretation is strengthened without overclaiming
3. **Bulletin of Mathematical Biology**

Do not simultaneously broaden the theorem and add biological anecdotes before TPB submission. The current advantage is that the paper has a sharp, auditable claim boundary.

## Impact upgrade path

A future empirically qualified genotype-policy system could change the target class. The decisive upgrade is not another abstract theorem. It is evidence that a biological system actually supplies:

- the declared modular/heritable representation;
- the relevant gain map;
- local mutation coordinates;
- enough mutation-measure information to make an occupancy prediction meaningful.

If that bridge closes prospectively, reassess Evolution / broader evolutionary-theory venues. Until then, TPB is the best fit for the theorem that actually exists.
