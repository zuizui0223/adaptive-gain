## One-sentence contribution

For an explicit finite branch-product genotype-policy representation, exact layer multiplicities reduce all suboptimal competitors to one adjacent obstruction, yielding distinct exact thresholds for aggregate modality and stationary majority under standard weak-mutation stationary weighting.

## Scope sentence for editor

The paper is a population-genetic theory result about how a specified genotype–phenotype representation changes stationary population occupancy; it does not claim a new fixation process, a universal weakest-link law, or empirical qualification of the routing representation.
