# Routing second paper — manuscript spine v1

## Status

Working manuscript architecture for the routing population-theory paper. This surface is intentionally isolated from the frozen flagship `manuscript/` directory.

The paper must stay narrow. It is **not** a synthesis of PRs #15–#33 and it is **not** a new origin-fixation framework. Its theorem-level center is the exact aggregate gain-layer transition generated by one declared finite routing representation.

## Working title

**Finite routing representations create sharp selection–multiplicity thresholds**

Alternative, slightly more mechanistic:

**Representation-induced multiplicity shifts the stationary gain mode of finite routing architectures**

Avoid titles built around “weakest-link epistasis”, “free fitness”, or generic “selection versus entropy”; those literatures predate this paper.

## Paper-level question

A finite routing architecture may have a unique maximally adapted genotype but many more genotypes at slightly lower gain. How strong must selection be before the **full-gain class**, aggregated over genotype multiplicity, becomes the dominant stationary gain class?

The paper asks this only for a declared finite branch-product routing representation, then shows exactly which parts of the conclusion disappear when the representation or neutral mutation measure is changed.

## One-sentence result

For the finite routing space

`X={0,...,q}^k`, `g(x)=min_i x_i`,

with symmetric per-gain stationary tilt `theta`, the full-gain class becomes aggregate-modal exactly at

`theta_c=2^k-1`;

for `k>=2`, equality produces a two-layer tie `{q-1,q}`, and strict inequality above the threshold makes the full-gain class uniquely modal.

This is an **aggregate gain-layer result**, not an individual-genotype mode switch.

# Result hierarchy

The paper should contain three mathematical results and two scope controls. They are not five equal headline theorems.

## Main Result 1 — exact finite routing-layer multiplicity

Let

`X_{q,k}={0,...,q}^k`,

and

`g(x)=min_i x_i`.

For gain layer `r=0,...,q`,

`D_r = (q-r+1)^k - (q-r)^k`.

Equivalently, if `s=q-r` is distance below full gain,

`A_s=(s+1)^k-s^k`.

The full-gain layer contains one genotype. The adjacent layer contains

`A_1=2^k-1`

genotypes.

### Role

This theorem turns the routing representation into an exact genotype multiplicity profile. The formula itself should not be advertised as a novel order-statistic identity in the abstract; the contribution is its exact role inside the declared finite routing genotype-policy map.

## Main Result 2 — all lower layers reduce to the adjacent-layer obstruction

Define

`T_k=2^k-1`.

Then for every `s>=1`,

`A_s <= T_k^s`.

For `k>=2`, equality occurs only at `s=1`; for all `s>=2`,

`A_s < T_k^s`.

### Proof certificate

Map each `x in {0,...,s}^k` with `min_i x_i=0` to

`S_j={i:x_i<j}`, `j=1,...,s`.

The `S_j` are nonempty and nested. Removing the nesting constraint gives `(2^k-1)^s` arbitrary strings of nonempty subsets. At `s=1` there is no nesting restriction; for `k>=2,s>=2`, nonnested strings exist, giving strict inequality.

### Role

This is the combinatorial engine of the paper. It converts a comparison against every lower gain layer into one adjacent-layer constant.

## Main Result 3 — exact aggregate-layer modality trichotomy

Under a symmetric stationary tilt `theta>0`, each genotype at gain `r` has weight proportional to `theta^r`, hence aggregate layer weight

`W_r=D_r theta^r`.

For `k>=2`:

1. `theta<T_k`: the full-gain layer is not aggregate-modal;
2. `theta=T_k`: exactly `{q-1,q}` tie for maximal aggregate stationary weight;
3. `theta>T_k`: the full-gain layer is uniquely aggregate-modal.

The proof is immediate once Main Result 2 is available:

`W_{q-s}/W_q = A_s/theta^s`.

### Population-size corollary

For the declared Moran multiplicative fitness step `a>1`, standard origin-fixation theory gives

`theta=a^(N-1)`.

Therefore

`a^(N-1)>=2^k-1`

is necessary and sufficient for the full-gain class to be aggregate-modal, while

`a^(N-1)>2^k-1`

is necessary and sufficient for unique aggregate modality.

For canonical routing `k=q+1` and `a=2`, both first occur at

`N=q+2`.

### Role

This is the paper’s headline theorem. The new object is the exact architecture-dependent aggregate-layer threshold, not the standard origin-fixation weighting used to convert fitness into `theta`.

# Prior-art bridge — lemma, not contribution

The population-genetic machinery should be introduced briefly and explicitly as established prior art.

Under weak mutation and reversible neutral mutation measure `mu`, the selected stationary law has the standard form

`pi(x) proportional to mu(x) theta^g(x)`.

Under the symmetric neutral measure used for the main theorem, `mu` is constant on genotypes and the layer mass is proportional to raw multiplicity times `theta^r`.

Do not present any of the following as new:

- Moran fixation probability;
- `rho(R)/rho(1/R)=R^(N-1)`;
- Sella–Hirsh / reversible stationary weighting;
- neutral-measure or sequence-entropy factors;
- minimum / weakest-link fitness maps;
- generic competition between state abundance and selection.

The closest contemporary comparison currently frozen in the novelty audit is Labourel, Bansept & McCandlish (2026), who analyze weakest-link `min` epistasis under mutation-selection-drift. The manuscript must distinguish their continuous component/order-statistic problem from the present finite routing-layer theorem.

# Scope Control 1 — representation dependence

This should be the first and visually strongest boundary result, but not a coequal headline theorem.

Hold fixed:

- gain levels `{0,...,q}`;
- phenotype-level fitness `W(g)=a^g`;
- population-level tilt `theta`.

Compare:

### Branch-product representation

`X_prod={0,...,q}^{q+1}`, `g=min_i x_i`.

Full-layer threshold:

`theta_prod=2^(q+1)-1`.

### Compressed gain-chain representation

`X_chain={0,...,q}`, one genotype per gain.

Full-layer threshold:

`theta_chain=1`.

Thus the same phenotype levels and fitness schedule can give opposite stationary gain-layer conclusions solely because the genotype-policy representation differs.

The canonical `q=2, theta=2` witness is especially clean:

- compressed layer weights `(1,2,4)`, full mass `4/7`, full layer uniquely modal;
- branch-product multiplicities `(19,7,1)`, weights `(19,14,4)`, full mass `4/37`, full layer nonmodal.

### Role

This prevents the paper from accidentally selling `2^k-1` as a universal biological threshold. It is an exact consequence of a declared representation.

---

# Scope Control 2 — mutation-bias / neutral-measure dependence

Even a fixed support graph does not identify stationary occupancy without the neutral mutation measure.

For reversible mutation measure `mu`,

`pi_x proportional to mu_x theta^g(x)`.

On a fixed gain path `0<->1<->...<->q`, any strictly positive target stationary distribution `p` can be realized by taking

`mu_r proportional to p_r theta^(-r)`

and constructing a reversible proposal kernel with that neutral measure.

### Role

This is a claim ceiling. It shows that the symmetric raw-multiplicity threshold is conditional on the mutation measure as well as the representation. Do not promote generic mutation-bias nonidentifiability as a new theory of mutation bias.

# Main-text section sequence

## 1. Introduction — representation can oppose a unique optimum without changing its fitness

Motivate the gap between per-genotype fitness ranking and aggregate phenotype/gain occupancy. State immediately that abundance/entropy effects and weakest-link maps are established; the unresolved object here is an exact threshold induced by a finite routing representation.

End with three questions:

1. How many genotypes occupy each routing gain layer?
2. Can all lower-layer comparisons be reduced to one obstruction?
3. Under what exact selection tilt does the full-gain class become the aggregate stationary mode?

## 2. Model — finite routing genotype-policy map

Define `X_{q,k}`, `g=min`, symmetric stationary tilt, aggregate layer weights, and the crucial distinction between individual-genotype probability and layer mass.

Keep Moran/Sella–Hirsh machinery to one compact prior-art subsection.

## 3. Exact layer multiplicity

Prove Main Result 1. Use `q=2,k=3` as the running example:

`D=(19,7,1)`.

## 4. A global combinatorial bound

Prove Main Result 2 with the nested-subset-chain certificate. This section should be short and proof-forward.

## 5. Sharp selection–multiplicity transition

State the trichotomy and population-size corollary. This is the conceptual center of the paper.

## 6. The threshold is representation-specific

Use the compressed chain counterexample. Do not broaden into a survey of genotype–phenotype maps.

## 7. The threshold is mutation-measure-specific

State the reversible neutral-measure boundary and one fixed-support contradiction witness.

## 8. Discussion

Focus on four implications:

1. a unique optimum need not be the dominant aggregate class;
2. finite architecture can make the adjacent suboptimal class the exact obstruction controlling every lower class;
3. representation and mutation measure are causal inputs, not nuisance implementation details;
4. biological application requires an empirically defensible genotype-policy map and mutation model.

Do not claim that real organisms generally use the branch-product representation.

# Biological claim ceiling

The paper may say:

> For a declared finite routing representation, genotype multiplicity can create an exact architecture-dependent selection threshold for which gain class dominates stationary occupancy.

It may not say:

> Natural regulatory, neural, or genetic routing systems generally have threshold `2^k-1`.

Moving from the formal routing representation to a biological system requires, at minimum, independent evidence for the genotype-policy representation, local mutational moves, and neutral mutation measure. The existing biological qualification program is therefore complementary evidence, not a premise silently assumed by this paper.

3. Model: finite branch-product routing genotype with weakest-branch gain.
