# Stage79 | Strong-gust macroecology of long- vs short-range floral visual information: authenticated source power audit

Date: 2026-11-18 JST. Original research question: whether wind **gust extremes**, not mean wind, rearrange the relative ecological usefulness of information available before and after floral approach to observed *Bombus*. This is continental/macroscale, **not island biology, not photo-colour sampling, not another one-species experiment.** Branch stacks directly on the independently verified Stage78 strong gust study source PR #157 and Stage77 observational flora×Bombus research.

**Source signal stage is a hypothesis, not a measured behavioral sequence.** Species-level flower-petal width from botanical source prose is a partial proxy of a distal visual display dimension, not genuine flower total display/contrast measured in the field. Positive botanical marking terms are not measured UV nectar guides and unreported terms are UNKNOWN, never negative. A published flowering-age color-change positive report is a literature source-positive phenotype, but does not confirm an early/late flower at an EuPPollNet site at the observation time. Wind is a remotely modelled ERA5 10m maximum daily gust for source-recorded visitor calendar months, not the actual wind encountered in a bee landing.

## Why the first source-gust size test is NO-GO (not no biological effect)

Pre-fixed feasibility criteria required at least 25 source informative networks, ten independent study IDs, and at least five studies with within-study strong-gust variation. The sample had 23 networks and 4 wind-varying studies, hence **NO-GO** for an independent strong-gust × petal-width slope. The original pooled source null-local log width score ratio was **−1.571**, but this is **NOT a fitted preference slope, p-value, evolutionary effect, proof of preference against large petals or evidence of wind moderation**. All missing insect guild records remain unknown, not biological zero.

**Correct next source optimization (Stage79I):** freeze the **original 392 petal-width-contrast networks themselves** and sample up to 5 network-year records per original study, using only original study, source site/year/month and spatial diversity before observing original Bombus or ERA5 strong-gust values. Re-fetch source ERA5 actual daily gust15 for this width-first panel, then calculate the same original strictly recorded bee network-null scores and assess study variation. Source study January-only records are conservatively excluded pending validation because defaulted January dates are possible in some publications. This optimization is objective exposure/site design, not searching across many models for a preferred positive association. Results are only valid after genuine GitHub Actions success.

## Prospective mechanistic test to preserve

> Strong gust episodes may reweight **the decision stage** at which floral visual information affects observed Bombus floral channel choices.

But this **cannot yet be concluded** by comparing descriptive network/plant trait co-occurrence, and it is not an established novelty merely by combining known colour, nectar guide, floral age, odor or landing mechanisms.

A testable distant-vs-near optical prediction must have (i) positive and directly measured signal variants with genuine negative/unchanged states where required, (ii) measured receptor-specific recognition distance or independently valid stage allocation, (iii) study/site abundance and visit effort/detection corrections, (iv) source-timed strong wind and other climate covariates, (v) independent clades/studies. Current source literature lacks true measured pattern/FCC negatives, and multiple source taxa are repeatedly sampled across many networks. Wind×VOC mediation and actual flower-landing costs also lack compatible same-site source measures. Scent literature is supplementary prior art, not a fitted macro mediator.
