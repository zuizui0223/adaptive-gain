# Stage107 — WorldClim long-term mean wind × floral reporting bias across all 72 source exploration mainland regions

**2026-11-20 JST.** Source-locked spatial sensitivity, NOT the original predeclared 1991–2020 strong-gust >15m/s test, NOT a biological adaptive signal-order result. Draft stacked onto Stage106 PR #185; main unchanged.

## Why WorldClim can help, but cannot substitute the primary wind exposure

Stage105 used authenticated original source first50 WCVP TDWG3 ERA5-model 1991–2020 **daily max gust >15m/s at ONE representative regional site**, with only 33 sites within the 72 source geometry contact-free mainland exploration region cohort. The apparent original NHM textual marking rate vs gust point correlation was rho +0.679, but after rank-correcting for original flower-colour/WFO botanical documentation and latitude/temperature was only +0.082; source documentation coverage itself was strongly climate-associated. These are evidence-discovery and ascertainment diagnostics, not a confirmed physical plume, learning or landing mechanism.

To assess whether this discrepancy was an artefact of the first33 nonrandom TDWG source alphabetic subset, Stage107 uses the separately author-sourced global Stage87C **WorldClim v2.1 1970–2000 long-term MEAN WIND m/s at 10 arc-min climate grid**, originally available across 350/367 global flora source regions, with an original 48 candidate point sample per source polygon (NOT exact land-area polygon integration).

WorldClim MEAN wind and ERA5 daily 10m 3-second GUST >15m/s are distinct physical phenomena, resolution, temporal reference, and provenance. Their numerical slopes MUST NOT be compared as if matched climate variables; the Stage107 source is solely an independent physical-climate **secondary sensitivity exposure**.

## Explicit separate floral outcomes

- Original NHM flower-colour-field positive pattern WORD rate per native plant; separately per flower-colour-described plant. Neither is independently observed true petal contrast/nectar guide prevalence.
- Original WFO human botanical floral fragrance positive textual mention rate per all full source flora descriptions; separately among explicit positive + explicit negative original reports. The two denominators are not comparable real odor emission prevalences, and unmentioned is NEVER an explicit biological absence.
- Original AusTraits source-labelled flower_diameter species-level median within each regional WCVP native flora, with actual reporting >=3 and >=10 species support; not a plant-local phenotype or visible long-distance attraction experiment.
- Source ascertainment covariates: NHM flower-colour documentation completeness and WFO full floral description coverage; source original latitude, WorldClim baseline temperature and precipitation used only as descriptive rank-correlation sensitivity controls.

Results are raw rank Spearman rho and partial rank correlations after 2 source-reporting denominators or after those two plus latitude, temperature and precipitation. DO NOT report naive IID p-values for spatially dependent and taxonomically repeated source species. These are not preregistered sign tests or an independent species-evolution / bee choice-order result. The source WorldClim 48 points per TDWG region are not true climate area means and original WFO original descriptions are not equally ascertained across regions.

## Critical decision

A source positive wind-to-floral pattern report association that changes sign/magnitude under botanical documentation denominators is evidence that the **observation process is not ignorable**. It does not disprove a real biological wind effect, nor prove selection for any cue. A sensitivity on WorldClim mean wind cannot replace a tested 1991–2020 ERA5-CDS 10fg gust frequency across 125 original mainland region actual land area.

The next physical data task after Stage106 is actual Copernicus CDS-derived ERA5 daily statistics from 1-hourly original 10fg gust and 2m daily mean temperature over grid cells, weighted by independently source-masked geodesic land-intersection, with a small authenticated original source period and observed NetCDF schema audit preceding the full 30-year run. Stage106 independently verified source geographic 0.25-degree land weights over three small mainland regions; CDS climate output is still UNOBTAINED.
