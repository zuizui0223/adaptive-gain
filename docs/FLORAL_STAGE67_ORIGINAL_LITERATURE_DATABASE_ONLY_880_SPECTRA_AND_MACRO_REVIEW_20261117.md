### The crucial source access limitation

Anonymous REST download of original Dryad file bytes answered HTTP 401 authentication required. Source metadata and its SHA256 fields were accessible but original XLSX/CSV ZIP bytes were **NOT actually obtained or numerically analyzed**. The source-authentic retrieval script retains SOURCE_401_OAuth_REQUIRED and must never misreport downloaded raw samples. The public research article's reported 98 flower spectra or 64 Erica species are original article sample frames, NOT observations extracted from blocked raw bytes in our pipeline. Independently published one-region study data do not become 41 independent globally sampled continental replicates.

## Originality and external studies: why simply colour x pollinators is insufficient

- Dalrymple et al. 2020, doi 10.1111/nph.16737, already tested 339 native Australian flower spectral plants, 17 sites, 11 abiotic/biotic context variables, pollinator diversity and climate/solar. Mean flower colour vs bee richness is not novel.
- Coetzee et al. 2026, doi 10.1111/1365-2435.70276 and Dryad 10.5061/dryad.6djh9w1gz: 33 bird-guild and 31 insect-guild Erica species; bird-pollinated flowers were less conspicuous to bees, yet field nectar robbing was not directly linked to bee conspicuousness. Receiver-avoidance vs receiver-attraction and optical visibility vs achieved fitness must be distinguished. These are *published* findings, not rerun by Stage67.
- Xie et al. 2025, doi 10.1016/j.biocon.2025.111322 and Dryad 10.5061/dryad.612jm64gq: 41 island plus 16 mainland localities, source spectral plant colour, real bee visits and source 98 coded plant sample spectra; already examined island fragmentation × bee hue visitation.
- Lawson 2017, doi 10.1098/rsos.170996, already demonstrated visual backup in windy odour interference; Cell 2026 doi 10.1016/j.cell.2026.10.005 reports regulatory coupling of nectar-reward, UV nectar guides and floral scent. Generic multicue cooperation is not sufficient novelty.

## Macro biological hypothesis after source correction

Across independent, wild and source-origin-verified plant lineages, which receiver-guild availability predicts **which visual information modality**: spatially structured UV or visible petal guidance, within-flower receptor-specific contrast, flowering reward/age state, or anti-visitor optical avoidance? Do these predictions survive common climate/UVB/light, range, sampling, phylogenetic and study-source effects, and differ for bees, butterflies, flies and birds?

Required source grain is one original named botanical taxon/population/study and independently measured source optical organ/band or true negative, matched to native source site/accessible region and source documented visitor or modelled guild pool. Separate raw VOC from wind pollination mode, and meteorological wind from ecological selection. A visual feature with no UV sector measurement is UNKNOWN, not spatial UV negative; review absence is UNKNOWN, not no flower colour change.

Stage57 source 2016 UV-pattern species x predicted Bombus share had strong climate-latitude confounding and unstable genus control; 2016 source genus quasi-separation cannot be fixed by an AIC table. Do not fit a cross-study causal SEM from unmatched species/locality/year data, and do not claim wind×VOC×UV fitness without same sampling population.
