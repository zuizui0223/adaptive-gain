# Stage57 | Floral visual information × receiver guild × shared environment: source-first macroecological evidence and identifiability

Status: 2026-11-17 JST. Central scientific goal: **separate environmental filtering, receiver-guild availability and inherited floral traits**, without treating a cross-sectional prediction as pollinator selection or calling every UV-reflecting flower a nectar-guide-positive species.

## Research question and competing paths

A floral visual trait may be informative to different receivers, but environmental UV-B, temperature, rain/water stress, elevation, flowering season, phylogenetic ancestry and local light can influence both the trait and the guild distribution. The test is not 'Does flowering UV correlate with Bombus richness?'; it is:

> After controlling for the same plant ancestral background, sampling geography, climate, soil/light context, observer effort and native receiver species pool, does local guild availability predict specific independently measured visual traits (spatial UV guide, visible guide, receptor contrast, age/reward colour change), and which stages of behaviour mediate effects on real visit/contact/pollen?

**Association audit, not a causal mediation SEM:** E -> B; E -> V; hypothetical B -> V; ancestry+native region -> E/B/V. Historical selection on V cannot be identified from 2026 modelled bee ranges and undated species-mean UV phenotype. For causal mediation P would require temporally ordered exposures, no unmeasured E/B/V confounders, local cooccurrence, and interpretable selection mechanism. A directed graph is a hypothesis until those conditions hold.

## Actual original-source UV-pattern × predicted Bombus × climate preliminary fit

Author source Koski & Ashman (2016), DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.13921. Source genus climate by 159 GBIF-accepted UV-phototyped species (81 original-directly-measured uniform/78 patterned), Stage55 independent original source bee-model coverage (10,242 original HEX; 2,695 source-eligible HEX, 2026 author tree and PA threshold), same 159 accepted GBIF taxon IDs.

Stage57 original run: https://github.com/zuizui0223/adaptive-gain/actions/runs/44006869792 ; six mock-only tests passed. Source-endpoint N=106 species (>=5 qualified first-page GBIF plant records and >=2 eligible HEX each); 54 UV-patterned and 52 directly UV-uniform. **88/106 are Potentilla**; 7 Ivesia, 5 Drymocallis, 2 Horkelia, four other singleton genera. Drymocallis 5/5 are source-measured UV-uniform.

Observed exploratory two-equation results:
- E := original published mean species UV-B + temperature + precipitation. B := ratio of equal-visited-HEX mean modelled Bombus names / mean all bee names. OLS `B ~ E` R² = **0.182882**; E coefficients in standardized units: UV-B +0.347539, temperature -0.418083, precipitation +0.132394. B is a modelled source-name fraction, not abundance or visitation.
- Original author species-mean UV-B and published latitude are highly correlated **r = -0.869721**. Do not interpret UV-B, altitude and latitude as independently identified causal forces simply because they are entered in separate regressions.
- Binary `UV_pattern ~ E` exploratory GLM AIC **154.02068**; `UV_pattern ~ E + B` AIC **153.75672** (delta +0.26396; virtually no model selection improvement). Gamma linear mediation or a product-of-coefficients is not warranted for binary V and mismatched temporal/spatial grain.
- **Quasi-complete separation:** the four-category source grouped genus nuisance model (Potentilla, Ivesia, Drymocallis, Other) has an all-uniform Drymocallis cell and showed genus/intercept standard errors around **13,000**, despite yielding numeric AICs 147.67/148.13. Those finite-looking genus-adjusted AICs are **INVALID FOR INFERENCE AND COMPARISON**. Subsequent script patch treats these models as `QUASI_SEPARATION_DO_NOT_COMPARE_AIC`, returning AIC null rather than suggesting phylogenetic adjustment works. An ordinary genus random intercept with only eight species-unbalanced genera is also untrustworthy.
- The prior Stage55 nonoverlapping within-genus climate/native-L3 matched 24 valid pairs gave strict-tier average modelled Bombus fraction patterned-uniform **+0.003894**, median **-0.001495**, positive 7 / negative 17, leave-one-pair-out sign-fragile. Neither a robust positive association nor a verified biological null is established.

All measurements are from original-study species means, native broad L3 botanical areas and bounded opportunistic 2000–2026 GBIF indexed pages, NOT the measured botanical voucher's location or original bee visits. **No p-value or adjusted causal field effect, plant fitness or reproductive success** is supported.

**Research gap** is not merely the association of flower colour with pollinator diversity: Dalrymple et al. (2020) already evaluated that. New claim would require *optically distinct information channels*, original species-level source receptor model, matched locality-native guilds, source ecology and phylogeny, and stage-specific functional contact/reward/pollen evidence rather than lumped human colour.

## Minimum future structural models (registered as hypotheses, not presently fitted)

1. Regional/source-site environment layer: `guild_available (bee, butterfly, fly, bird species-specific model) ~ climate_pc + UVB + elevation + habitat + landscape + native_reach + region` with model support, no true zero outside domain. Climate projection, effort and source model errors carry through.
2. Phylogenetically informed visual layer: `source_measured_spectral_or_guide_trait ~ environment + guild_available + original_genus/clade_phylo + spatial_field + source_study + plant_occurrence_quality`; continuous and binary visual outcomes modeled separately, observational paths not declared causal.
3. Receiver contrast layer: evaluate measured flower-part UV/vs visible guide and receptor-resolved `DeltaS` or receptor colour discrimination, matched to the appropriate visitor guild; bee source-name richness is not general bee colour-system selection.
4. Field functional layer: actual visitor contacts `~ cue × guild × wind/flower_motion + phenology + abundance`; seed set/pollen receipt requires source experiment or field observation on same phenotypes.
5. Falsification: compare direct E->V fit to receiver-augmented alternatives; preregister taxon/native/locality controls and spatial/phylogenetic held-out contrasts; stop if missing original specimen location, source study or lineage independent replicates.
