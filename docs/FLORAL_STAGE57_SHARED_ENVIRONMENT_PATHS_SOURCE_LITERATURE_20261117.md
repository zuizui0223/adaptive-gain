# Stage57 | Floral visual information × receiver guild × shared environment: source-first macroecological evidence and identifiability

Status: 2026-11-17 JST. Central scientific goal: **separate environmental filtering, receiver-guild availability and inherited floral traits**, without treating a cross-sectional prediction as pollinator selection or calling every UV-reflecting flower a nectar-guide-positive species.

## Research question and competing paths

A floral visual trait may be informative to different receivers, but environmental UV-B, temperature, rain/water stress, elevation, flowering season, phylogenetic ancestry and local light can influence both the trait and the guild distribution. The test is not 'Does flowering UV correlate with Bombus richness?'; it is:

> After controlling for the same plant ancestral background, sampling geography, climate, soil/light context, observer effort and native receiver species pool, does local guild availability predict specific independently measured visual traits (spatial UV guide, visible guide, receptor contrast, age/reward colour change), and which stages of behaviour mediate effects on real visit/contact/pollen?

**Association audit, not a causal mediation SEM:** E -> B; E -> V; hypothetical B -> V; ancestry+native region -> E/B/V. Historical selection on V cannot be identified from 2026 modelled bee ranges and undated species-mean UV phenotype. For causal mediation P would require temporally ordered exposures, no unmeasured E/B/V confounders, local cooccurrence, and interpretable selection mechanism. A directed graph is a hypothesis until those conditions hold.

## Minimum future structural models (registered as hypotheses, not presently fitted)

1. Regional/source-site environment layer: `guild_available (bee, butterfly, fly, bird species-specific model) ~ climate_pc + UVB + elevation + habitat + landscape + native_reach + region` with model support, no true zero outside domain. Climate projection, effort and source model errors carry through.
2. Phylogenetically informed visual layer: `source_measured_spectral_or_guide_trait ~ environment + guild_available + original_genus/clade_phylo + spatial_field + source_study + plant_occurrence_quality`; continuous and binary visual outcomes modeled separately, observational paths not declared causal.
3. Receiver contrast layer: evaluate measured flower-part UV/vs visible guide and receptor-resolved `DeltaS` or receptor colour discrimination, matched to the appropriate visitor guild; bee source-name richness is not general bee colour-system selection.
4. Field functional layer: actual visitor contacts `~ cue × guild × wind/flower_motion + phenology + abundance`; seed set/pollen receipt requires source experiment or field observation on same phenotypes.
5. Falsification: compare direct E->V fit to receiver-augmented alternatives; preregister taxon/native/locality controls and spatial/phylogenetic held-out contrasts; stop if missing original specimen location, source study or lineage independent replicates.

## Sampling and structural identifiability note

Selection of 106/159 original 2016 taxa by plant observation counts and bee-model eligible hex coverage may create a **sampling/collider bias**: observer effort and geography jointly affect both sample inclusion and modeled bee-guild exposure. This is on top of inherited genus, regional climate and source-phenotyped specimen mismatches. Adding genus to a regression does not solve this when genus×trait cells are separated. A credible causal path model needs matched source/population phenotype, real/predictive native receiver pool with matching time/area, phylogeny+environment, and explicit detection/source eligibility as a selection model. Until then fit associative sensitivity only, not `indirect effect = a*b` as causal mediation.

## Deeper full-text checks: environment is also a moderator of perceptual access

The fixed 126-paper DOI/PMID source search was followed by independent article full-text inspection, **not** used to fabricate new species-level traits.

- Finnell & Koski 2021, *New Phytologist*, [doi:10.1111/nph.17631](https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.17631): **Argentina anserina** has small-guide preference at low elevations and larger-guide preference at high elevations. Actual experimental ambient/reduced UV and bare/foliage floral backgrounds showed high-elevation pollinators prefer large guides under ambient UV but preference fades in reduced UV; flies prefer large guides on bare background. This is primary evidence for **environment × optical-signal × receiver interaction** (sensory drive), not simply environment's confounding effect on receiver geographic ranges. This genus/species overlaps Potentilleae lineages; not a new independent clade added to the 817 source atlas merely because paper is different.
- Todesco et al. 2022, *eLife*, [doi:10.7554/eLife.72072](https://doi.org/10.7554/elife.72072): wild sunflower UV ligule patterns relate both to pollinator preference and to drier environments, with UV-absorbing flavonol glycosides and altered desiccation resistance and HaMYB111 regulation. **Abiotic protection/water conservation can generate the same visual phenotype variation** as receiver selection. The competing mechanisms should be separately modelled and tested.
- Ortiz et al. 2021, *Functional Ecology*, [doi:10.1111/1365-2435.13709](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2435.13709): actual community source **98 plant species**, measured visual cues and pollen/nectar rewards, evaluated source phylogenetic dependence. Their original study reports flower *size* as the strongest honest reward signal, and nectar concentration related to chromatic contrast. Colour alone was not a universally predictive reward signal; signal utility differs by behavioural stage and reward. This is community-local, not global flower guild distribution.
- Rodríguez-Castañeda et al. 2024, *Plant Biology*, [doi:10.1111/plb.13702](https://doi.org/10.1111/plb.13702): two sympatric yellow UV-bullseye versus white UV-patternless floral morphs of **Anemone palmata**, 2018–2020 in two south-west Spain populations with source reported coordinates, spectrometry of apex and base regions, UV photos, observed 282 visits (252 yellow, 30 white), observed preferential visitor guilds and greater female fitness for yellow morph. Source measured **visible colour and UV pattern change together**, so a flower-morph effect cannot be ascribed to UV spatial guide alone without factorial manipulations. A valuable functional field **case study**, NOT a phylogenetically independent macroecological replicate.

### Revised path/interaction hypothesis and identification requirements

Separate two environment branches:
- **Abiotic filtering:** macroclimate, drought, UV-B, altitude, native range, ancestral genome -> directly shape source UV pigment allocation, optical absorbance and physiological protection.
- **Signal environment/sensory drive:** ambient spectral irradiance, canopy/bare background, optical clutter, flower age/reward state -> change *receiver-specific* detection and interpretation of the same measured flower reflectance pattern.
- **Receiver source pool:** macroclimate/geography -> species-specific available bee/fly/butterfly/bird guilds (potential receivers) -> actual flower visitor community only after phenology, access and observed behaviour.
- **Functional interaction:** visual phenotype V × local optical environment E_light × receiver guild R -> actual detection/landing/contact/pollen/reward visitation conditional on co-occurrence. Wind/flower motion later enters as access cost, not the sole visual mechanism.

The key additional path is an **interaction** `V × E_light × guild -> observable response` rather than only a simple linear `environment -> Bombus -> V` product. A historical selection mediated pathway into V is not identifiable without temporally matched, phylogenetically independent original populations/selection data. Existing Stage57 106-source taxa + Stage55 modelled HEX cannot test this sensory drive interaction because actual receptor model, background light and phenotyped source-site information are absent. Experimental demonstration in Argentina is evidence that the mechanism *can* occur, not that it explains our 817 source taxa.
