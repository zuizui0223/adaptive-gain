# Stage85 — Source-verified 50 taxa and phylogenetic replication gates

2026-11-18 JST. Purpose: independently measurable flower-age optical contrast across original taxon species, NOT to confirm wind-driven bee cue-order change by counting database records.

## Observed original source coverage

- Authentic original Ohashi 2015 285,933,377-byte spectrometer ZIP, with source MD5 and SHA256 locked by original Stage79/Stage85 receipts.
- Stage84 original DoPI Version 2.0 strict taxon roster: 50 of the 180 original simple binomial Ohashi spectrometer species occur in DoPI, comprising 28 previously receptor-modeled exact-name taxa and 22 additionally measured taxa.
- Actual re-extracted source: 2,263 valid independent specimen spectrum workbooks from all 50 taxon names, 1,360 among previous 28 and 903 among added 22. The source forms 86 species×flower-part groupings; only 82 have all ten early and ten late measured specimens.
- Precisely 49/50 species have at least one complete original early/late part pair, including 21 of 22 additional taxa. The original Firmiana simplex part has ten late measurements but no early counterparts and is NOT a source-proven no-colour-change case. Other incomplete parts concern Houttuynia cordata and Laburnum anagyroides, while Lonicera japonica compound e/m author filenames remain unresolved rather than being assigned to early or mid stages.
- The 50 source taxa span 46 genera, compared with 26 prior genera. New taxa add 20 non-overlapping genera; this remains fewer phylogenetically independent contrasts than 49 taxa.

## Source visual-model contract

The additional 22 must use the exact same original pavo Apis mellifera model used for prior 28: D65 illumination, green foliage background, von Kries adaptation, and source 300–700nm measured reflectance. FAR means green photoreceptor achromatic/background contrast, not actually calibrated flight detection distance. NEAR means bee hexagon colour/background contrast, not direct nectar guide, learning, approach or landing measurements. The original author publication Ohashi et al. 2015 already established green-versus-chromatic stage dynamics and phylogenetic signal, so merely repeating those findings is not novelty.

Do not compare old 28 BH q-values across 53 parts directly to new BH q-values across the new full 50-species part family. Old 28 exact original continuous receptor metrics must reproduce numerically before admitting new 22, and all source below-detection/complete contrast states must be kept separate from missing early/late groups. The original author reported 48/219 bee-visible species; that published count is not the classification of the DoPI-matched 50.

## Macroecological evidence gate

Original DoPI contains 676,900 rows and exactly 50 original Ohashi matching source taxa; only 2,925 rows involve the additional 22. Original rows are not independent observations: 148 repeated source Record identifiers with identical selected content are flagged by Stage84. Source observed visitors, pollen-confirmed plant pollination and independent plant fitness are distinct outcomes.

Before fitting optical trait by recorded Bombus-vs-other guild networks, freeze source species, require shared independent original publication + dated site + habitat, source survey effort semantics, explicitly identified pollinator taxon, local plant availability, phylogeny and regionally native plant scope. Use both publication and plant genus/species leave-outs; repeated visits to one taxon do not multiply independent phenotypic origins. DoPI positive visit records cannot identify local zero-visitation probabilities. Different source DOI strings may still reuse the same underlying site or study.

None of the 50 author source spectra is paired to the European DoPI visitation event flower or visiting bee individual. No original source measures actual wind at visitation, flower-odor plume intermittency, prior discrimination learning, approach/landing kinematics and seed fitness simultaneously. A cross-database mediation SEM would therefore be unjustified. Claims about wind-driven ordered information access remain mathematical hypotheses, not a new corroborated macroecological result.
