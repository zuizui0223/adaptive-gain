## Real source data-quality audit

- Original DoPI record labels are **not globally unique**: **148 repeated labels** among 676,900 rows, all with identical source publication, plant, animal, time/location, interaction count and pollination-code fields under the independent content digest; zero conflicting selected-content collisions. This is approximately 0.022% of rows. No rows were silently removed; before any visit-rate analysis, explicitly deduplicate or otherwise account for these 148 repeats with a declared observational denominator. The existence of repeated identical database rows is not proof that a real bee never revisited the same flower.
- For the existing 28 receptor taxa, 39,814 source records have source methodology values **DO=38,615**, **PA=1,163**, **NA=36**; the original labels must be interpreted against the author field descriptions before pooling actual direct visit and pollen-inferred records.
- Original pollination evidence grades: grade 1 confirmed **66** rows; grade 2 inferred with attached pollen **3,280**; grade 3 circumstantial visitation **36,284**; **184** have unavailable or nonstandard grade values. These 66 grade-1 rows do not identify behavioral cue-order, seed fitness or independent population responses. Grade meanings follow the author's DoPI evidence rubric.
- The corrected original EuPP source has 52 publication keys represented as 54 Study IDs, and 18,798 plant×network rows. Our intentionally strict binomial parser retains 1,395 source EuPP species names versus 1,408 original accepted species-ranked labels; excluded source names are unresolved under that tighter nomenclature rule, not absent plant species.

A full macro analysis still needs the 22 newly discovered species' author stage-resolved source spectra converted to a declared receptor model; independent study, plant phylogeny, sampling effort and actual contemporaneous flowering stage need auditing.

There is no same-flower co-measurement of VOC plume turbulence, wind, Bombus learned recognition decision, flower movement and landing/pollination fitness. No causal SEM or false floral adaptiveness claim is estimated. Next evidence priority: the 22 source taxa first, followed by truly independent original site/hour behavioural evidence, rather than mining more conditional signs in repeated-source observational networks.
