## Source-only result

Globally, original botanical prose source is matched exactly to 27,899 accepted WCVP native species. After removing cross-organ text false positives (e.g. leaves fragrant, flowers white), text classifier distinguishes 420 floral-fragrance-positive *lexical candidates*, 105 explicit-negative candidates and 27,374 unmentioned/uncertain. **These are not manually verified smell/odor emission states.** Original GIFT mode of 420 global positive candidates: 36 animal, 2 wind, 382 unknown, not an independent study of receptor preference.

For the **125** strictly mainland TDWG3 regions:
- 114 regional floras contain >=1 fragrance-positive report candidate and 90 contain >=1 explicit negative; 89 contain both; **only 8** contain 5+ of each.
- Among 37 frozen mainland holdout regions: 33 have >=1 positive, 29 have >=1 explicit negative, **28 have both**, only **3** have 5+ of each.
- Among 125 mainland regions, **23** have 10+ source-positive marking mentions, 10+ true AusTraits labelled flower diameter species and 10+ scent-positive report candidates in *separate* response-specific denominators. Only **4** of the 37 fixed holdout regions do. **This is NOT 10 species with all three signals co-measured!**
- Mainland 125 regions contain 1,488 repeated positive fragrance species-region incidences versus 302 explicit-negative incidences, drawn from a much smaller source identity pool; geographic repetitions are not independent traits or evolutionary transitions.

## Scientific decision

The global botanical scent extraction finally provides **explicit positive AND explicit negative** lexical observations across multiple major floras, a material improvement over NHM's absent scent field and positive-only marking. But genuine fragrance prevalence still cannot be identified by treating 27,374 unmentioned source descriptions as negative. Descriptive source mentions are conditional on botanical attention and plant/source identity; the 105 explicit negative source species provide an uneven documented-negative benchmark only.

Do NOT fit 3-cue complete-case species response (single matched VOC+size+pattern set would collapse). Preserve separate species denominators and a common region/block split. Only after the original text's human precision/recall audit, externally verified original gust 1991–2020 regional land-area exposure, botanical documentation/canopy and clade controls and spatial source lineage checks may conditional spatial source associations be estimated. Long-distance bee odor attraction, actual individual visual guide use, sensory information order, learning or plant fitness are NOT tested.

Stage95 strong-wind pilot 1991–2020 true daily reanalysis model fetch succeeded at 5 original mainland exploration sites and 3 cold mainland sites; it is still **point-sampled Open-Meteo ERA5-derived daily gust**, not original CDS hourly 10fg land-area regional averaging. Stage95C expands only source validation, not causal interpretation.
