# Aedes material request outreach packet v1

## Status

Planning-only. This packet prepares the shortest material-acquisition contacts needed to execute the frozen Aedes program. It does not send any message and does not modify the frozen scientific or execution-planning semantics.

## Priority order

1. NPYLR7 agonists — highest priority / highest current risk.
2. Ir68a two-allele package — second priority.
3. LVP baseline — public catalog path, verify exact parental pedigree.
4. Ir8a causal lines — defer until Stage B is justified.

Dear Dr. Duvall,

I am preparing a preregistered replication/extension of the nutrient-independent NPYLR7 host-seeking suppression result in *Aedes aegypti*. The experiment is deliberately staged so that the pharmacological state perturbation must replicate in a Liverpool/LVP background before any downstream genetic integration is attempted.

Would it be possible to obtain research-use aliquots of two independent active NPYLR7 agonists from the 2024 study, ideally TDI-014188 plus one of TDI-014184/TDI-014186? If compound transfer is not possible, would you or the TDI medicinal-chemistry team be able to share a synthesis/resynthesis route or analytical specification sufficient for a local core facility to reproduce the compounds?

Thank you very much for considering this request.

## 4. Ir8a line request — deferred Stage B

### Likely scientific contact

Carolyn (Lindy) McBride
Princeton University
Public contact: `csm7@princeton.edu`

Published Orlando Ir8a resources include independent `Ir8a^attP` and `Ir8a^DsRed` alleles. More recent Aedes genetic-tool papers state that IR8a strains/plasmids are available upon request, and `pBB-AaIR8a` is deposited as GenBank `PQ671724`.

### Do not contact yet unless Stage A passes

The current planning freeze explicitly defers same-background Ir8a genetics until a candidate A/B routing signal survives the cheaper LVP screen.

When Stage B becomes justified, the request should ask for:

- the two independent Ir8a alleles used for causal host-cue validation;
- current Orlando parental stock information;
- genotype verification protocol;
- permission/advice for backcrossing or recreating the allele in LVP;
- any updated IR8a driver/knock-in resources that make a common-background causal stack simpler.

## 5. Contact outcome receipt

For every outreach, record only one of:

- `AVAILABLE_DIRECT_TRANSFER`;
- `AVAILABLE_WITH_MTA_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_PROCESS`;
- `RESYNTHESIS_OR_RECREATION_REQUIRED`;
- `UNAVAILABLE`;
- `NO_RESPONSE_YET`.

Do not treat `NO_RESPONSE_YET` as `UNAVAILABLE`.

For the two NPYLR7 compounds, Phase 1 cannot start until two compounds are in one of the first three states with a concrete material path.

## 6. Hard stops

- do not send a broad “any compounds you can spare” request; preserve the preselected-compound rule;
- do not request extra Ir68a or humidity-receptor alternatives as backups;
- do not order a different Liverpool strain simply because it is easier to obtain;
- do not begin Ir8a line construction before Stage B is triggered;
- do not use silence/non-response as biological evidence against feasibility.
