# Aedes domestic LVP route v1

## Status

Logistics/pedigree planning only. This file is downstream of `freeze/aedes-execution-readiness-v1` and cannot alter the frozen scientific task, execution rules, or material decision criteria.

## Executive conclusion

A domestic Japanese route to a Liverpool baseline is now credible enough to test **before** arranging a new international BEI shipment.

However, “Liverpool” is not one interchangeable stock. Obihiro currently/persistently documents at least two biologically distinct Liverpool lineages:

- `LVP-OB` — the original Obihiro Liverpool lineage;
- `LVP-IB12` — BEI `MRA-735`, the highly inbred IB12 lineage.

These must not be conflated.

The highest-value domestic question is therefore whether the **MRA-735 / LVP-IB12 material used at Obihiro in the 2024 Scientific Reports study is still maintained or recoverable**.

## 1. Garrity/Tang reference background

Tang et al. 2024 uses `wild type (LVP)` as the comparator for the Ir40a/Ir68a knock-in lines and states that mosquito rearing/behavioral assays followed the preceding Garrity-lab protocol.

The preceding Laursen et al. 2023 Neuron key-resource table identifies its wild-type Liverpool (LVP) as obtained from the **Leslie Vosshall laboratory**.

The Vosshall laboratory mosquito-rearing SOP explicitly describes its Liverpool wild-type colony as `Liverpool (LVP-IB12)`.

Therefore the relevant pedigree question for the Ir68a package is whether the knock-ins were generated/maintained on that Vosshall/Matthews LVP-IB12 lineage.

This is strong protocol-lineage evidence but not yet a substitute for direct confirmation from the Garrity lab.

### Garrity pedigree question

Ask:

> Were `Ir68a^EYFP` and `Ir68a^RFP` generated and maintained on the same Vosshall/Matthews LVP-IB12 background used as the wild-type comparator in the preceding hygrosensation work? If not, what exact parental Liverpool stock should be used as the comparator?

Do not call the background `LVP-IB12` in the final empirical receipt until this is directly confirmed.

## 2. Obihiro has a domestic IB12 route distinct from LVP-OB

The 2024 Obihiro Scientific Reports study explicitly used six mosquito strains including:

- Liverpool (LVP)-Obihiro (`OB`);
- `LVP-IB12` (`IB12`);
- crosses between them.

The paper states that:

- `OB` is the original Obihiro D. immitis-susceptible Liverpool lineage;
- `IB12` is a non-genetically modified line obtained from BEI Resources as `MRA-735`;
- OB and IB12 differ in D. immitis susceptibility/refractoriness.

Therefore `LVP-OB` and `LVP-IB12` are empirically distinct laboratory strains and cannot be substituted for one another merely because both are Liverpool-derived.

## 4. RIKEN BDR route

RIKEN BDR's Metabolic and Behavioral Physiology team currently uses *Aedes aegypti* and studies the transition between host-seeking and reproductive behavioral states, making it the strongest domestic **scientific-fit** candidate for the frozen H↔O question.

Recent RIKEN work states that its mosquitoes were derived from the Liverpool strain and provided by Ryuichiro Maeda / Obihiro.

That wording alone does **not** establish that the RIKEN colony is LVP-IB12. Historical Japanese papers using Maeda-derived mosquitoes describe a Liverpool-origin colony, while Obihiro now separately names its original lineage `LVP-OB`.

Therefore current RIKEN Liverpool must be treated as:

`LIVERPOOL_DERIVED; IB12_STATUS_UNRESOLVED`

until pedigree is confirmed.

If RIKEN's current colony is LVP-OB rather than IB12, it remains valuable for assay development but cannot silently replace the frozen baseline comparator.
