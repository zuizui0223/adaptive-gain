## New independent original source
Debeuckelaere et al. (2026), `Data and code from a self-medication experiment: pathogen exposure-dependent foraging decisions of bumblebees`, KU Leuven RDR Dataverse, dataset DOI **https://doi.org/10.48804/UIIVLP**, public v1.0, **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. The project independently sampled **18 colonies (six controls, six fungal exposure, six bacterial exposure)**, 5 days per colony, automated robotic flowers offering a control nectar or the phytochemical quercetin, and **more than 54,000 recorded visitation events**.

Public original files include `data_selfmed.csv` (~6.9 MB, publisher MD5 `3a3578d61d7633635615d43829df3f3c`), `00_README.md` (MD5 `b05dd7324c909f8f65d95ae938c68aca`), `Data_analysis_selfmed.R` (~56 KB) and solar records. **These MD5 values are provider listing labels, not yet independently verified original bytes.**

The robotic flowers record infrared-triggered feeding events. There is **no assumption** that a visiting bee is individually identifiable; the event stream may be *colony-wide* rather than animal trajectories. Source row count, actual `bee_id` field if any, `flower_id`, clock, colony ID, reward treatment, source day and trial order must be inspected before fitting any individual route model.

## Scientific analysis plan to freeze after source schema, before empirical endpoint
If source records colony/time/reward-type and day, primary new generalization question: Does the **temporal dependence of colony-wide use of alternative artificial flowers** persist after conditioning on experimental colony, reward-type availability, time-of-day/day and pathogen treatment? An adjusted model must use colony-disjoint replication or colony-level bootstrap, not treat 54,000 events as 54,000 independent colonies. **Do not model individual bees' behavioral route, direct flight heading, floral accessibility or adaptive sensory query order.** Predefine once actual schema identifies event grain and support; report no-go if event timestamp/date/colony/reward labels are not linkable.

### Strong interpretation ceiling
This is a separate original study of colony-level nectar preferences with treatment-driven physiological state, **not a new test of field wind, direct insect flight inertial heading or natural plant-plant competition**. Even a positive colony memory/time association cannot establish adaptive routeability decision costs `C_A,C_F`, fitness or species-level universal behavior. The original experiment's scientific question was pathogen-triggered quercetin choice, already described by authors; replication of that headline alone has no novelty.

Keep PoMS Stages3e–g, original Stage4–6 heldout geometry results, and frozen V5/V6 manuscripts unchanged. This new source audit is isolated in a draft research PR.

### Freeze the NEW independent colony-level temporal-organization endpoint before computation
**Scientific question:** Does use of the two reward options display **minute-scale temporal persistence within original experimental colonies**, beyond both local 60-minute reward composition and instantaneous observed event volume? This is *not* individual bee route memory. It is intended as an external process-scale comparison to the single-bee Stage4–6 result.

Native intervention does **not** manipulate flower movement, ambient wind or actual individual sensory trajectories. One independent experimental **colony**, not one sensor event, is the independent replication unit.

**Fixed signal and time scale:**
- Use exactly the independent reproduction of the paper's **54,362 clustered and filtered events**, no additional event class chosen by results.
- For each original `(colony, recorded date, hour of day)`, form **twelve non-overlapping 5-minute bins**. Each bin contains total recorded original *visits* `n` and quercetin visits `q`, with reward label derived only from the publisher's `trt` `CONTROL/D1`. No arbitrary animal-level event ordering, fabricated individual track ID or across-day boundary.
- A bin is eligible if **n≥3** distinct grouped sensor events. An original hour is eligible if **at least four** of its twelve bins are eligible. Within such an hour, consider a pair of bins only if their 5-minute indices differ by exactly 1 and both are eligible. **No bridging a missing/low-detection bin or separate hour.**
- Compute per-bin smoothed quercetin share `p=(q+0.5)/(n+1)` and per-colony mean absolute adjacent-bin share difference, `D_colony = mean_pairs |p(t+5min)−p(t)|`. Small D indicates local temporal *similarity*, but is not a bee behavior state or reward preference mechanism. Denominators are original eligible bin pairs, not the 54,362 source rows.
- Primary pooled statistic is **unweighted mean of D over qualifying colonies** (not event-weighted), with **≥10 eligible adjacent-bin pairs per qualifying colony and ≥8 qualifying distinct original colonies**. If support fails, emit `NO_GO_COLONY_LEVEL_HOUR_MATCHING` and do **not** tune bins, time windows or pair thresholds to obtain a positive result.

**One source-fixed negative-control permutation:**
- **199** pseudo-random permutations, fixed RNG seed **20261028**. Within every original eligible `(colony,date,hour)`, randomly permute the original **whole (n,q) bin records** among the *same eligible five-minute positions*; bins with n<3 and their locations remain fixed. This preserves (i) colony/date/hour group, (ii) exact flower-visit totals and quercetin totals in every 60-minute block, (iii) all eligible versus ineligible 5-minute positions, (iv) exact source distribution of bin sizes and binary reward shares, and (v) the number of *valid adjacent* bin pairs in each colony. It deliberately removes the actual five-minute order of those group-local choice-intensity bins. **No shuffling of individual bees is implied or possible.**
- Primary effect `Δ_D = mean_colony[D_randomised−D_observed]`; **positive Δ_D means more observed smoothness** than the constrained hour-level null. Positive-tail exact MC p `(1 + # shuffled D ≤ observed D)/(199+1)`, minimum 0.005. Report permutation distribution, all individual colony D and pair count, positive versus negative colony effects, and **unweighted** 18-colony-level summary. It cannot identify whether the cause is robot replenishment, local activity bursts, individual revisits, colour learning, social facilitation, or sensory availability.
- Predeclared descriptive sensitivity: compare the same `D` calculation on all 18 colonies versus eligible-only, but do not draw inferential claims from sparse colonies; report counts excluded. Report presence of original source same-device 2s merges and source paper's original unequal flower availability.

**No claims of causality or replication of individual cognitive order:** previous studies' 2019 single-bee route-history prediction is on tagged individuals; here the source is **18 colonies, without bee tags**, so a positive value is colony-resource *temporal aggregation*, not new evidence that the same bee remembers its last flower or that flight inertia/visual query order causes reward choices. The same source already studied pathogen and reward preferences; repeating its treatment main effect is not our analysis objective. Stop with positive/null as frozen; no minute-window grid search or species subset mining.
