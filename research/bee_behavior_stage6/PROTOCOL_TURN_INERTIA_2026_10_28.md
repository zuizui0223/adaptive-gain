# Stage 6 — bee route turning inertia versus learned previous-flower history
**Freeze time:** 2026-10-28, **BEFORE** Stage 6 original-data direction-model results are inspected. Exploratory as a whole (follows Stage 4 and Stage 5 positive findings).

## Question and nontrivial alternative
Stage 4 original ten-flower bee data showed held-out previous-flower predictive information beyond fixed bee-level flower preference. Stage 5 using original publisher Figure 2 coordinates showed true physical distance explains part of it, but the residual history prediction of the *destination when switching flowers* was heterogeneous (5/10 bees improved). A plausible third confound is **straight-line travel continuity / heading persistence**: arriving from flower A to B geometrically changes what next flower C lies ahead, even if the bee has no source-dependent memory or information-query policy. Test the **bearing of two consecutive source-level flower-to-flower steps** and its effect on genuinely held-out prediction.

## Immutable source and observation grain

- Exact publisher Supplemental Dataset S1: 61,316 bytes, SHA256 `0d30b0957c4b18602beebf60a915e03bee26142a8593f252badd77881b5e9dfe`. 881 original foraging bouts, 6,205 visit slots, 5,324 *within-bout* adjacent pairs; 10 marked bees, source days 1–5.
- Actual ten physical artificial flower positions in metres from publisher Figure 2, verifiable `research/bee_behavior_stage5/flower_positions_figure2_verified.json`, original publisher PDF source SHA256 `7f275a219fe0128d30f70cf6d812ab41d4b64727a740102549e8362b83d4ff8b`.
- Earlier day 1–4 training, held-out entire day 5 (never training/hyperparameter selection), exactly 10 bee tags; 1,550 day-5 original within-bout adjacent *pairs* remain the source anchor but are **not** the denominator for the new triplet endpoint.

## Triplet definition, never impute a route
An eligible physical-direction event is a sequence of **three consecutive visit slots within one original foraging bout** `A→B→C` such that `A≠B` and `B≠C` (two actual *between-flower* steps). No crossing nest returns, bouts, days or individuals; no extra flight-path interpolation or individual landing timestamps. The observed turning angle is approximated solely by the **two straight-line endpoint displacement vectors**, not a measured aerial heading: `v = position(B)-position(A)`, `w = position(C)-position(B)`, `cosθ = (v·w)/(|v||w|)`. Counts of cosθ positive/near-zero/negative and source meter distances reported by bee, with valid test counts and complete sample ID/hash reconciliation.

*Repeat* pairs and triplets with `A=B` or `B=C` are excluded from **this predeclared direction-only estimand**, not from Stage 5 main results. For candidate C, restrict only that `C≠B`; no hard visibility cutoff and no zero probability outside a 9.6 m radius. Directional model is a geometry approximation, not direct measured heading or cue access.

## Locked train-only model set and primary estimand
For every test triplet `A→B→C`, compute destination probabilities conditional on actual switching `C≠B`, with all nine physically possible destinations:
- `Mgeo`: frozen Stage 5 `P_b(C|B) ∝ static_bee(C)×exp[-λ d(B,C)]`, `λ=0.05/m`, static bee margins trained only on days 1–4 with **α=0.5**. Verify this frozen value remains the unique winner of Stage 5 training grid `{0,.025,.05,.1,.2,.4,.8}` before scoring.
- `Mgeo+history`: Stage 5 first-order learned transition counts per bee, smoothed **β=5** toward Mgeo, *without* using direction. This is the previous Stage 5 historical model, now scored on triplet-eligible subset.
- `Mturn`: start from Mgeo's **switch-conditioned** destination distribution over nine alternatives, tilt by `exp(κ cosθ(A,B,C))`, re-normalize among nine candidate physical destinations. **One global nonnegative κ per turn score**, selected using **only days 1–4** training triplets by pooled conditional negative log likelihood. Locked candidates: `{0,0.25,0.5,1,2,4}`, tie → smallest κ. `κ=0` reproduces Mgeo.
- `Mturn+history`: start from the *already trained* Mgeo+history switch-conditioned distribution, tilt by **the same** κ and candidate `cosθ`, renormalize; *no re-estimated κ*, *no extra bee-specific free parameter* and no test-day training.

All four models are evaluated on **exactly the same held-out eligible triplets**, with positive predictive probabilities, same source bee tags, same earlier data and source-fixed 10 flowers. Primary discriminator is **Mturn vs Mturn+history**: bee-equal average proper log-loss improvement `NLL(Mturn)−NLL(Mturn+history)` on day 5, **not** an all-transitions statistic that could be inflated by same-flower repeats. Report 10 per-bee effects including negative ones. A simple within-one-colony exact paired sign flip is descriptive, not a population-level p-value. Predeclared minimum: ≥5 eligible triplets per bee and ≥5 eligible bees; otherwise NO_GO.

Secondary exact matched contrasts: Mgeo→Mturn (heading contribution), Mgeo+history→Mturn+history (heading with history), Mgeo→Mgeo+history on this same triplet subset. Report full per-bee NLLs, training candidate grid losses and test-source triplet counts, without cherry-picking `κ`, a flower subset or test group after inspecting scores.

## Predeclared conditional null
The heading-specific negative control permutes the **incoming previous flower A** among eligible day-5 triplets that share the same bee and the same current flower B, keeping each observed true current→next flower B→C event, each bee's source flower-frequency margins and physical distances, the per-bee previous-flower-with-current counts, and all trained probabilities fixed. Recompute **Mturn vs Mgeo** held-out bee-equal gain on shuffled incoming A. Fixed **199 shuffles**, fixed seed `20261028`. Report how many individual triplets belong to groups of size >1 and changed incoming labels per shuffled replicate; fail closed if fewer than 100 day-5 eligible triplets could even be permuted or <5 bee tags have at least 5 such cases. This *tests within-source association between incoming direction and next source flower* under a restricted shuffle, not randomly manipulates bees' sensory states and not independent colony replication.

## Scientific ceiling
All bee routes in these raw data are **ordered flower IDs, not measured continuous flight tracks**. Position triplet vectors are chord/endpoint approximations only; nearby visual orientation and angular inertia are not directly observed. The source experimental system is **one bee colony, one flower array, five days, no wind manipulation**. Improvements may arise from fixed geometry, direction, landmark placement, behavioral routines, individual preferences, rewards or unmeasured perception and need not show cognitive information acquisition. No nectar fitness, interspecific interactions, randomized cue availability or causal effects `C_A/C_F` are identified.
