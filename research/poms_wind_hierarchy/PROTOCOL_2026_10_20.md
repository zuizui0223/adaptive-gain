# Wind, guild-by-guild capture and cross-taxon hierarchy: pre-result analysis contract

Date: 2026-10-20. Exploratory original-data extension of the same-site/day PoMS work. Biological goal: distinguish effects of wind on capture/visitation, potential correlated responses of otherwise unrelated groups, and actual incremental site-transfer predictive value from presumed ecological interactions.

## Primary source and design
- UK Pollinator Monitoring Scheme, FIT 10-minute original data (EIDC DOI 10.5285/d706088c-9d48-4be5-b156-6ed6ad5e0618) and independently collected pan-trap station/insect/flower data (DOI 10.5285/5841bfae-471b-498c-af89-c30e5140d6c5). Use frozen member SHA256s; never infer unsurveyed flowers are zero.
- Wind measurement: FIT leaf-motion categories (ordinal 0/1/2); pan traps average of deploy/retrieve Beaufort scores (ordinal 0–5). Do not imply m/s or hourly continuous wind. Evaluate recording agreement, not physical calibration.
- Conditional within-site-date trap contrasts: undisturbed 4–8h individual station observations; 12 guilds, offsets for trap hours, station position and vegetation, local illumination and temperature. Cluster uncertainty by entire original site; BH across 12, and check flower-list completeness/99th-percentile extreme count exclusion.
- Contextual cross-guild associations: fit all guild wind/microhabitat models, compare residual co-capture correlation among 66 unordered guild pairs between predefined calm and brisk categories, site-cluster bootstrap, BH across pairs. No statement of interspecies competition/facilitation from residual correlation.
- Cross-layer prospective comparison: exact site/date FIT–pantrap joins (no nearest-date interpolation); baseline covers target flowers, weather, time, focal capture and measured flower richness. Add nonfocal guild captures, then a wind × nonfocal-context term. Evaluate new-site Poisson log loss via fivefold whole-site holdouts. Fix penalties 0.025, 0.25, 2.5 before examining outputs.
- Seasonal wind check: 5–6 versus 7–8 month at same site/year/flower category, exactly same eligible strata for M3/M4/M5; add FIT leaf wind and then pantrap Beaufort deltas. Bootstrap whole sites, refit all covariates.

## Decision / stop rules
A mechanism claim needs consistent across-data/out-of-site incremental evidence, robustness to measurement and common-support choices, a measured interspecific interaction rather than co-capture, and independently observed available flower alternatives or prey/hosts. p or q on a correlated observation alone does not demonstrate a causal biotic edge. A wind×flower category pattern with structural separation is no-go for ecological promotion. Do not tune more variants simply to obtain significance.
