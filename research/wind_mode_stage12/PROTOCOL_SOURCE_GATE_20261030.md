# Stage12: wind, co-occurring insects and honeybee movement mode — original source gate

**2026-10-30.** Continues the Stage11 study with a *different, directly observed* response: flights vs walks between flowers, not the existing Stage11 nectar interaction. This is a fresh hypothesis on an existing observational field dataset, not a new field experiment and not independent source replication of Stage11.

## Original data and grain
Harris, Ratnieks & Balfour 2026 DOI 10.1111/1365-2435.70429; Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.9kd51c608 version 456836. Reuse the original-provider SHA256 verified `forage.csv` (1,227 60-second observed foraging rows, 123 survey IDs), `weather.csv` (127 measurements, 122 survey IDs), and `insect_surveys.csv` (123 source survey-level community snapshots). Do NOT join the original behavioral events one-to-many with the two or more wind readings. Aggregate to an original survey, retaining original row counts, invalidities and mismatches in a receipt. C52 is not intrinsically excluded here: the absent direct nectar measurement is irrelevant to this endpoint. The `Prunus spp.` versus `Prunus avium` discrepancy from Stage11 does not authorize reclassification: insect IDs and dates, locations and original labels must be audited explicitly.

The 60s foraging record includes counts of `number of walks`, `number of flights` and `flowers probed`. Survey community counts include `honeybees` and numerous other named taxon **categories** (not a rigorously standardized species list). Wind `beaufort` is ordinal, not continuous metres per second. The original sampling selected patches where honeybees were sufficiently represented, and observations occurred during relatively favorable wind (<10 km/h) conditions.

## Required before any biological fit
1. Re-fetch *actual* author-original source through Stage11 checksum-gated provider workflow. Complete source-grain QA: unique insect survey ID, strict 1:1 source IDs across tables, exact observation dates and site/location semantics, duplicate and missingness checks, integer nonnegative original flight/walk counts, 60s observation semantics, total transitions=flight+walk, any impossible move counts versus probes, season/plant support, insect non-honeybee counts, and no survey-level row multiplication.
2. Show distributions **not statistical wind correlations** of recorded flight+walk totals, eligible survey counts, available independent city×dates, non-honeybee proportions, wind ordinals, and plant support. Retain all source technical anomalies in the receipt rather than guessing or retrofitting rows.
3. Only after QA, write a frozen single main biological estimand on the same original data, before fitting effects. Target: does `wind × co-occurring non-honeybee share` predict allocation between actual flying and walking moves (a descriptive compositional *association*, not demonstrated socially acquired cue use), controlling plant, city, meteorology, time and flower density. Outcome conditional on a recorded inter-flower move. Sampling-unit = survey; city×date cluster for dependence. If source QA is insufficient, do not fit.
4. Do not select favorable taxa, sites, weather ranges or regressions after effects. The result can be positive, null, or negative; precommit one primary endpoint and a no-go condition. Main, frozen Stage9b, Stage10, Stage11 and manuscripts unchanged.

**Separability:** co-occurring non-honeybee fraction is an observational community metric, potentially influenced by wind, and cannot be interpreted as a randomized heterospecific *information cue* or a fitness consequence. The observed fly-versus-walk choice conditional on movement is not a full available-target set, and source data have no persistent bee tag or colony ID.

Current deliverable: a source-verified structural audit; no biological wind/heterospecific coefficient is authorized by this first gate.
