# Wind across ecological opportunity modalities: source-locked comparative test

Registered 2026-10-21. This is an **exploratory reanalysis**, not a prospective confirmation of already observed UK PoMS or orchid-bee findings. Frozen V5/V6 and main are unchanged.

## Main question
When wind is related to pollinator observation, distinguish **how many encounters** occur from **which species are observed**. Contrast male orchid bees approaching scent baits, UK PoMS FIT flowers/pan traps, and (if feasible) independently observed wind-turbine insect behavior. Wind units, detection processes, taxa and environments differ; do NOT pool as one causal wind effect.

## Original Crall et al. source and frozen checks
Source 10.5281/zenodo.3675327; original `CrallEtAl_OrchidBeeWindEcology.zip` published MD5 `42e53512e4bdad2701593a89d1c87dfd`.
The file has 70 unique site/date/scent wind sessions across 9 sites (including recorded zero captures), with recorded session counts summing **409**. The `beedata.csv` specimen table has 450 specimens because 41 records belong to 7 site/date/scent keys not found in the matched wind-session table. Exactly 67 wind sessions have positive specimens, and 3 have verified zero; do not mistakenly use all 450 specimens.

1. **Wind and abundance:** within-site mean wind versus session count, with source site, month and scent control; inspect between-site separately. Report log link model, site clustering (only 9 clusters), within-site/month shuffle test (4999 reps), cineole-only and site deletion. No positive hypothesis selected after reading effects. This general abundance result has been studied in Crall et al.; it is not new original ecological evidence.
2. **Wind and composition:** whole-session vector of identified orchid-bee species, normalized to observed session totals. Compute global species–wind association and compare to 4999 permutations of wind labels **within site×month**, preserving whole session species vectors and counts. Zero-catch sessions are kept for count tests but cannot define species composition. Report eligible sessions, blocks and richness; no arbitrary per-species multiplicity surfing.
3. Cross-scale within-versus-between source comparisons are descriptive, not field causal identification of wind, forest cover or scent dispersal.

If not enough observed wind variation across >=10 location/date strata, declare a no-go for wind-mediated inference; do not invent it.

## UK PoMS and other-species interpretation
Retain previous original-data wind relative guild rates, cross-life larval trait RR≈1.62 (genus/lineage-confounded), and plant-record richness moderator NO-GO (622 vs locked 1000 trap gate; all five site-held-out folds worsened). UK PoMS measurement of 10-min flower visitors and 4–8h pan-trap catches is **not directly commensurate** with Costa Rican orchid-bee scent-bait counts. Predator/prey effects remain potential pathways until independent edges are observed in the same matched unit.
