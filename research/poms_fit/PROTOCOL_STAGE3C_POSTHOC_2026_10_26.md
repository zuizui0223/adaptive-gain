# Stage 3c exploratory falsification controls: wind-dependent guild composition
2026-10-26. **Post-hoc after Stage 3b positive bumblebee/other-fly composition finding.** Not preregistered confirmation.

## Locked question and target
The only headline contrast remains bumblebee/other_flies log odds per 10-minute focal-flower PoMS FIT census under strong wind versus calm/occasional-leaf movement. Stage 3b structured-site adjusted OR=1.973 (CI 1.401–2.779), Holm p=0.00030 over the earlier three comparator family. Stage 3c is a direct stress test of *that selected signal*, not a new multiplicity-controlled discovery. Do not reselect endpoints based on success.

## Three diagnostics to report irrespective of outcome
1. **Public FIT mode transfer:** Repeat exactly the same within-pair conditional binomial definition on the public FIT source, but with public survey adjustments country + habitat type (rather than 1km site fixed effects), wind, target, floral units, year, 4-df day-of-year spline and sunshine, SE clustered by recorded 1km square. Source-data member digest must equal bcd2ef780d3208a5a4aba5c191178900ccef705e5b569dc0160b27d00652dc3c. The two FIT modes belong to the *same monitoring programme*, not two independent countries or experiments.
2. **Top-1%-count influence** within structured FIT: remove *all surveys* with pair total above its precomputed 99th percentile, then refit the original exact binomial site-effects model. This prevents an arbitrary number of counted insects from one high-count observation driving the result. Do not truncate pair counts or change the cutoff after seeing fit outcomes.
3. **Coarse matched exposure** within structured FIT: group by (location_code, year, target_flower_corrected), retain strata containing at least one calm and one strong-wind census with >0 pair visits in both categories; drop gentle wind, use a stratified Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel bumblebee/other_fly 2x2 (strong/calm by guild) pooled-OR diagnostic. Report number of matched strata, sites, surveys and total insects; refuse estimate if <15 strata or <10 sites, and flag that animal visits within censuses and strata are not independent, so the canonical asymptotic CI/p-value is descriptive only. No relabeling of season as perfectly controlled is allowed.

## Interpretation
Absolute visit-rate and conditional composition are different estimands. A positive bumble/other-fly relative odds does *not* mean bumblebee visit rate increases, that flies and bees compete, that insects perceive wind as an information cue, or that wind influences actual flight direction. Specific plants are chosen by observer, wind is three observer-reported leaf-movement categories, and all unobserved alternative flowers are absent from FIT. The aim is stress-testing an exploratory association, not treating robustness as causal identification.
