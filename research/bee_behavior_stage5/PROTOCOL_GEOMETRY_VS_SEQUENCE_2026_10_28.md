## Frozen same-data evaluation once original coordinates are independently verified

All models trained on original source days **1–4**, score entire original day **5**; use precisely the same **881 bouts, 6,205 visits, 5,324 genuine within-bout adjacent flower pairs**, same 10 bees and 1,550 day-5 transitions. Do not create between-bout pairs. Source day-5 records NEVER affect the model-fit weights or geometry calibration. Work with the unrounded meter coordinates and ten physical labels exactly as printed.

1. `M0` static preference `P_b(next)`: earlier-day bee-specific visit count + Dirichlet α=0.5 per physical flower. Identical to Stage 4 M0.
2. `Mgeo` static bee preference *physical Euclidean distance*. `P_b(next | prev, λ) ∝ P_b(next) exp(-λ d(prev,next))`. **Global pooled λ** selected on days 1–4 **only**, maximizing proper conditional log likelihood across original within-bout training transitions. Candidate grid is fixed: **λ ∈ {0,0.025,0.05,0.1,0.2,0.4,0.8} per metre**, ties to smallest λ. λ=0 exactly recovers M0. Self visits are distance zero, but **do not** use a zero-probability "visible-only" cutoff.
3. `M1` bee-specific source transition counts by previous flower, smoothed by predeclared β=5 toward each bee's M0, identical to Stage 4 M1. Fit on days 1–4 only.
4. `Mgeo+history`: same bee-specific transition count shrinkage with **β=5** but shrink toward its bee-specific `Mgeo(next|prev)`, rather than toward M0. This is a practical nested predictive comparison, not a structural causal decomposition. Same train-only transitions, no heldout choice of hyperparameters.

Main unit for summary is *individual bee* (n=10 from one colony), with held-out mean **proper negative log loss** in nats per next flower. The **primary mechanistic discriminator is the bee-equal mean heldout conditional switched-destination improvement** `NLL(Mgeo)−NLL(Mgeo+history)` on observed `next≠previous` transitions, renormalizing all predictions to the nine alternative flowers and without changing trained models. The full 10-flower all-transitions difference is a secondary diagnostic, not the primary result. Compare (Mgeo−M1), (Mgeo−Mgeo+history), and (M0−Mgeo). Report signs of differences by bee and all models' heldout NLL; not merely one significance-threshold or a handpicked scenario. An advantage beyond physical distance is not proof of cognitive memory; paths, landmarks, direction, learning and flight constraints are unobserved.

### Separating movement from repeats
The all-transitions endpoint includes consecutive occurrences of **the same flower** in the source. Decompose raw 1,550 held-out transitions into self and between-flower, with counts by bee. Then report two predeclared descriptive sub-analyses: (a) **within-flower same-flower repeat** binary probability under each model; (b) **conditional distinct-flower destination log loss** on transitions with `next ≠ prev`. For (b), renormalize each model's 10-flower probabilities to its nine other possible destinations; estimate no extra parameters. **Require ≥5 heldout switched-flower transitions per bee and ≥5 qualifying bee tags** for primary inference, otherwise a clear NO_GO; do not redefine the threshold after seeing data. Report the bee-equal mean gain and number positive/negative. Any sign-flip p is a within-one-colony descriptive diagnostic only, not a cross-colony frequentist claim. Keep the original heldout population and per-bee sample sizes explicit. For fair comparison conditional on switching, M0 is also renormalized to nine other flowers. No retrospective deletion of actual repeat records from the main endpoint.
