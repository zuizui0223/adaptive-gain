# Wind persistence and uncertain encounter deadlines — preregistered model analysis

Status: 2026-10-23; **design frozen before producing numerical wind-persistence outcomes**. This is a mathematical counterfactual, NOT a field wind effect, encounter-based payoff or interspecific behavioural observation. Continues `zuizui0223/adaptive-gain` 32-world, two-heterospecific-cue cost-variable exact model.

## Setup and estimands

Hidden habitat branch B∈{0,…,15} and target T∈{0,1} independent uniform. Flower F_j returns 1 iff B=j and T=1, otherwise 0; flower query consumes 1 discrete time tick. Source A reveals branch bit0, B reveals bit1, both with 10% independent symmetric error and no marginal information about T; source observation costs differ by currently observed wind state: c_A=1 always; c_B=1 in calm, 2 in gust. Cost must be known when query is initiated. The wind state is observable immediately before choosing any subsequent query.

Time opportunity may close (e.g., flower/visitor departs) after each time tick. Two weather states C=calm, G=gust have per-tick persistence of the **encounter opportunity** s_C=0.95, s_G=0.65. Conditional on survival, wind transitions via symmetric 2×2 Markov R with P(stay)=p in {0.1,0.5,0.9}. Q=diag(s_C,s_G)·R is the substochastic joint one-tick survival/next-wind kernel. Marginal stationary weather frequency is 50/50 for all p, and mean survival probability at a randomly sampled stationary tick is 0.80 for every p. Starting wind distribution = (0.5,0.5), with wind-at-start-specific results reported separately. Different temporal correlations are NOT a claim that survival duration has equal distributions.

There is ALSO a hard cap K in {5,7} ticks. A sensor requiring c=2 is completed only if the encounter survives both ticks; if it terminates during acquisition, the subject receives no observation and must guess on the current incomplete information. Sensing errors and weather are independent conditional on B/T (no unmodelled species–weather mechanism). A strategy can stop/guess at any time, and may choose its next query depending on previous outcomes and *current* wind. Each cue usable at most once; opportunities are nonnegative so no reward for refusing to stop early except if it improves knowledge. No reward/time costs beyond stochastic deadline and hard cap.

The primary outcomes are the Bayes-optimal expected correct-target probability with no heterospecific cues, only A, only B, and both, for each K×p. Compute **A×B interaction** Δ_AB = P_AB - P_A - P_B + P_0, plus **adaptive-vs-precommitted** P_AB - P_F, and compare p=0.1 to p=0.9 holding stationary wind frequency and marginal source error rates fixed. P_F optimally chooses a noncontingent sequence of distinct flower queries without species cues, and is independently exactly computable as 0.5 + (Σ_{j=1}^{min(K,16)}Pr(survive j ticks))/(2×16). Contrast is mathematical information-policy advantage (not fitness).

## Precise dynamic program

Maintain binary outcomes for already-observed source signals, tested flower-counts within each of 4 source-bit groups (rather than 16-species mask), the current observable weather and K remaining ticks. Under the symmetric uniform resource prior, the 4 groups contain exactly four interchangeable flower branches each. The base correct-decision probability is 1/2 (always guess T=0 after all-negative flowers). Additional success comes only from a positive flower response when T=1; compute that surplus by exact Fraction arithmetic. Every action is discounted by the appropriate Q^c kernel. A source cue has outcomes 0/1 with latent joint error law; post-query weather and source-outcome mass factors are summed exactly. Enumerate every feasible source/flower action; no preselected ordering.

## Claim boundaries

The model measures probability of making the right *binary resource decision before termination*. It does not identify wind-dependent cue reliability, real interspecific interactions, pollination success, energy/time fitness, or true species richness scaling. A zero or negative Δ_AB is NOT evidence optional species information makes an optimized consumer worse; P_AB≥max(P_A,P_B,P_0) holds. Any positive wind persistence result needs calibrated wind Markov transitions, encounter closure hazard, and direct sensing histories before empirical promotion.

## 2026-10-23 addendum: latent versus announced fixed deadline (registered before numerical latent-deadline results)

Compare the same two-point exogenous hard deadline distribution K=3 (probability 1/2) or K=7 (probability 1/2), **mean K=5**, with K deterministically equal to 5. In an "announced" policy the organism is told K at the start; expected value is [V(K=3)+V(K=7)]/2. In a "hidden" policy it learns only whether the encounter continues after each action, not the realized K itself. It observes wind state after each completed query and can adapt the next cue based on its past readings. Hazard termination from weather remains Q; exogenous hard deadline is independently sampled. This is a **synthetic intervention on deadline uncertainty**, not evidence that bees know exact wind budgets.

A hidden-deadline Bellman recurrence uses P(K>=t+c | K>=t) as an additional completed-query probability, and retains weather Q^c. Stop/guess if the hard deadline or stochastic encounter loss occurs before a query finishes. With source cost c varying at action initiation, elapsed duration is tracked exactly; do not replace deadline by E[K]. Guarantee that P_announce >= P_hidden >= P_fixed for the same deadline mixture; the first inequality follows from information inclusion, the second because fixed floral acquisitions are feasible within the adaptive class. When there are no sources, P_hidden = P_announce = P_fixed and can be audited via the direct survival kernel. Under a single constant deadline, P_hidden=V(K) identically.

Report the full 4-source-context comparison for each of persistence p=0.1,0.5,0.9 and initial wind =stationary half-half. Compare A×B interactions of deterministic K5, announced mixture, hidden mixture; do not select p after viewing interactions. Secondary: test wind-at-start C versus G, but treat them as conditional interventions, not comparison of equal encounter duration distributions.
