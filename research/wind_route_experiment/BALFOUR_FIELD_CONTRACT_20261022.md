Scientific question: wind is associated with **inter-inflorescence transfer intensity** as distinct from flowers visited per observation and visitor identities. Source already published wind-linked increased movement between inflorescences; this is an integrity/sensitivity reanalysis, not new experimental finding. Wind is **observational**, not randomized, and original samples include only observed active bees, so wind-induced inactivity is conditioned away.

Primary endpoint (subject to schema/QC): inflorescences visited per flowers visited (counts) among source-recorded bees with positive total flowers and physically coherent inflorescence<=flower counts. Compare continuous wind m/s slope on logit/beta-like inflorescence/flower ratio or per-flower exposure-offset count model, adjusting bee species, flower species, date/location and temperature. The ratio is an operational index of inflorescence visits per observed flower, **not directly observed number of different plant individuals**. If counts lack coherent denominators or event units, NO-GO for ratio and report only independent count outcomes. Retain proper repeated-individual keys if available; absent identities require date×location clustered uncertainty. Keep full individual source n not counting observations as independent treatment replicates.

Secondary: flowers visited per fixed observation period vs wind, with flower species/bee species, temperature and locality, only if observation period is comparable and verified. Negative/positive signs across these endpoints do not constitute an experimental contrast or between-study causal meta-analysis.

Cross-system synthesis: controlled Burnett flight-tunnel randomized wind and obstacle height (Dryad `10.25338/B8Z34Q`) measures wind effect on flight motion and a median altitude-based obstacle-route proxy. The field movement dataset cannot be translated into this laboratory route. No real predator/prey interspecific causal edge is measured by either original archive. Publishing a novel ecological generality would need species-resolved replicated wind manipulation and observed partner-choice/availability.
