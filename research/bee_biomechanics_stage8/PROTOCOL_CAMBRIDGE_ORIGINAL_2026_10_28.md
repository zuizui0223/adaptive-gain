# Stage 8 — independent bees' flower biomechanics, reward, and ordered individual visits

**Protocol written 2026-10-28, before inspection of Cambridge original dataset or any Stage 8 outcome.** Exploratory study relative to prior Stage 4–7 work. Scientific target is **a second original experiment with individual bee ID AND experimental floral resource contrasts**, ideally separating movement/handling constraints from past choices. Original 2019 one-colony flower route data and 2026 18-colony aggregate sensor data remain untouched.

## Original source and evidence
Pattrick, Symington, Federle & Glover (2023), *Bumblebees negotiate a trade-off between nectar quality and floral biomechanics*, *iScience* 26:108071, paper https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2023.108071. University of Cambridge Apollo original open dataset **https://doi.org/10.17863/CAM.100572**, repository handle `1810/357732`, CC BY 4.0. The original publisher describes `TradeOffData.csv` as **ordered, millisecond-resolved bee behavioral observations** of visits, drinks, flights and rests, with three independently originating colonies, individual bee ID, randomized flower identity, foraging bout, flower orientation/surface difficulty, and high/low nectar sucrose contrast. Original `BeeMasses.csv` carries mass by bee and colony.

The paper's **published** result already explains nectar concentration vs difficult slippery flower handling using an *energetic rate maximization* interpretation. That finding is background, **not** a new Stage 8 discovery. Our new question is **whether an individual bee's immediately experienced floral handling event or previous flower visit predicts later decisions after static individual nectar/flower preferences and design treatment are accounted for**. The source may not encode the actual previously experienced reward; do not assume it does without first auditing the original event semantics.

## Frozen decision after source-role discovery, before Stage8 empirical outcome
If there are reliable distinct flower visit/handling records and a chronological sequence of actual visits per bee:
- **Primary descriptive endpoint:** assess whether immediately experienced **handling/friction event type** (e.g. difficulty/slipping, fly/land/drink as actually coded) predicts that bee's *next* choice between available flower types **after controlling for existing (colony,bee) preferences, experimental sucrose/concentration contrast and design features**. Train on earlier bouts, score later bouts; do not random-row split. Predeclare baseline vs one-step experience model and smoothing on inspected source measurement scale, never tuned on heldout choices.
- **Required controls:** exclude across-bout links; correctly distinguish flower *orientation* and *surface texture* (the source might describe only one column); distinguish **reward availability and received reward**; count action-episode diagnostics and repeated sensor events. Include explicit treatment assignments, randomized source flower ID, colony, bee.
- If no within-bee variation in experimental option types or no interpretable following choice, mark **NO_GO_HANDLING_SEQUENCE_IDENTIFICATION** and report descriptive source quality and biomechanical adjustment only. Do not replace the frozen test with a posthoc promising single subgroup.
- **At least 2 independent colonies, 10 individually tagged bees and 100 eligible actual forward flower decisions** required before descriptive summary; otherwise NO_GO. Even with 3 colonies the inference is conditional to these experimental source colonies, not a population-wide p-value.
- **No claims of individual visual sensory cue-query order, odor access, realized reproductive fitness, ambient wind, interspecific competition, or general source decision `C_A/C_F`.** The flower system manipulates surface grip/orientation and nectar quality in a lab, not wind or botanical diversity.

## Relationship to earlier empirical evidence
- Stage 4–6 independent original artificial-flower routes had **10 tagged bees but only one original colony**. Past-flower movement predicted future destination, but much of that signal was explained by geometry and incoming chord orientation; real flight trajectories/cognitive querying remained unobserved.
- Stage 7b had **18 independent colonies and 54,362 original author-filtered robot sensor visits**; found group reward-use temporal organization in 16 colonies, but **no individual bee IDs**.
- Stage 8 is intended to bridge those observational levels with individually tracked choices *across more than one colony*, while adding an independently manipulable **physical handling/reward access cost**. Any synergy/invariant cross-scale principle remains a hypothesis until same source records both individual actions and group response.

**Original file availability and data semantics take priority over model progress.** Freeze earlier PR #77/#79/#81 and V5/V6 manuscripts; do not overwrite prior claims or choose alternate hyperparameters to recover significance.


## Original-source role resolution (2026-10-28, BEFORE Stage8 outcome model fit)
The actual original Cambridge DSpace source was successfully retrieved from its `ORIGINAL` bundle and provider MD5 checks passed. The deposited original files are `Observation_data.csv`, `Bee_masses.csv`, `README_observation_data.txt` and `README_bee_masses.txt`, not the descriptive aliases `TradeOffData.csv/BeeMasses.csv` on the DOI landing page.

Source `Observation_data.csv`, **4,576,155 bytes**, original MD5 `c34200f5746389ac29a6f99b3c461819`, SHA256 `c38d18df02cb4feb492b86b0f4b5ca0c767388c69bac0be1b44262d0f39e7d33`: **61,592 recorded ACTION rows**, **38 bees, three source colonies**, nine original activity labels, only 38 missing final action durations. The publisher records **36 complete test bees**, excluding two incomplete bees `Yellow 4` and `Green 47`. `Bee_masses.csv`: 38 masses; the value for Pink60 was author-imputed. Original README descriptions were directly audited and are source-owning for action roles.

**Critical scientific corrected semantics:** `Time (ms)` is **action duration**, not an absolute timestamp. `Flower ID` is **a per-visit interaction-episode grouping surrogate** that is reassigned on a subsequent visit or after a rest; it is not a persistent physical flower position. It is thus not legitimate to build an A→B→C spatial geometry or return-to-physical-flower trajectory from these IDs. The original source row order for each identified bee is the recorded action order; do not sort by duration. Source `Foraging trip` defines actual nest-return boundaries.

An independent source-grain adapter reconstructed **12,064 actually touched flower visit episodes** from the 61,592 original activity rows: **4,817 easy coarse-rough familiarization episodes** and **7,247 slippery micro-rough test episodes**. Another 3,800 contiguous groups had no source classified flower action. **Zero** episode groups contain conflicting source horizontal vs vertical orientation. Original event class counts, 17 exact row duplicates *not discarded* (action records can legitimately repeat), and the 38 source-final missing action durations were preserved in the QA ledger.

Publisher source experimental reward assignments (confirmed against the original article): in `High Difference` treatment, vertical flowers **50% w/w sucrose**, horizontal **20%**; in `Low Difference`, vertical **50%**, horizontal **35%**; control **35%** on both. Source test surfaces are micro-rough/slippery (vertical generally requiring hovering), while coarse-rough easy-grip surfaces form the *earlier familiarization phase*, so neither surface phase nor reward differences were blindly treated as randomized within-bee instant treatments. The publisher already measured group-average preference and energy-rate outcome; our next test is a genuinely different **chronologically held-out, immediate prior outcome and next choice** question.

### Frozen Stage 8 paired-choice prediction before fitting empirical scores
**Primary population**: source author **36 completed bees** (original `Yellow 4` and `Green 47` excluded), each from one of the three original colonies; **micro-rough TEST phase only**. Include an actual source `horizontalLandVisit`, `horizontalLandDrink`, `landVisit`, `landDrink`, `hoverVisit`, or `hoverDrink` episode, first two source features:
- episode orientation `H` or `V` from the original observation label (not Flower ID, whose number is NOT a physical location);
- whether any `*Drink` action was recorded during the previous episode. **This is an observed drink action, not a guaranteed intake volume or nectar reward receipt**.

Construct source-origin ordered *consecutive* visit-episode pairs within each real `(colony, bee, Surface=Micro-rough, Foraging trip)`. Require no intervening source `rest` or `boutStartStop` observation between source episodes; do not bridge trips, another bee, surface phases or end-of-trial. Outcome is whether the next touched flower episode is **horizontal** (binary). Source returned actual physical options in a regular 15 horizontal/15 vertical grid, but available flower ID is per-episode, not spatial identity: this predicts *orientation of the next touched flower*, **not** A→B movement distance, individual sensory query-order or probability of drinking.

**Strict forward train/test**: for each bee, order all its micro-rough foraging trips by their first original row index; first **70%** of distinct source test-phase trips (round up, with at least one strictly later test trip) train, all remaining later trips are fully held out. If a test-phase trip has fewer than two valid touched flower episodes there are no matched next choices, not invented zeros. No random-row split and no hyperparameter tuning from test; each bee must have ≥10 earlier heldout-eligible within-bout transitions and ≥5 later ones to enter the descriptive forecast analysis. Require ≥10 bees and all **3 original colonies** represented, plus ≥100 unique true test transitions for the study endpoint.

**Fixed models, trained on earlier micro-rough source trips only**:
- `M0`: bee-specific unconditional preference to touch horizontal versus vertical flower: `P(nextH|bee)=(H_train + 0.5)/(all_train+1)`, 0.5 symmetric additive pseudo-count per binary option.
- `M1`: bee-specific `P(nextH|previous_orientation)` from actual earlier-bout transition counts, **β=10** pseudo-observations shrinking toward M0. This controls for orientation constancy/current experimental reward treatment and stable individual flower-type preference implicitly because treatment is fixed per bee.
- `M2` **PRIMARY predictor**: bee-specific `P(nextH|previous_orientation, previous_observed_drink_action)`, with **γ=10** pseudo-observations shrinking toward M1. The additional information carried by *whether the previous touch included a successful drink action* is the only new feature. No history from another bee and no presumed objective reward volume.
- Main proper score: bee-equal mean heldout **log-loss improvement M1−M2**, in nats/actual next flower orientation. Report **all individual bee scores**, colony-equal mean (3 source colonies), number positive/negative, time/test-period support and matched M0−M1. Any inferential p or confidence interval using 36 bees as independent colonies is prohibited; 3 source colonies are the external replication ceiling. 
- **Predeclared mechanistic sensitivity**: among test transitions where previous episode was **vertical and recorded hovering**, compare exactly the same M1 and M2 probabilities, without refitting or changing support. This separates handling of difficult flowers from generic all-episode orientation dependence; do not use it as main result if sparse. Require ≥10 bees and ≥100 test vertical-hover events; otherwise NO_GO for this sensitivity. Do not retroactively change these gates if effect is absent.

Interpretation firewall: A positive M2 effect could be a **behavioral state or reward-action sequence association**, but not a causal cost of motor hovering, optimal energy return or a proof of information-access hierarchy. Horizontal/vertical flowers were regularly alternated in space, but actual physical flower ID/coordinates are not observable in this original source; residual path geometry is **uncontrolled**. Source colonies n=3, tested bees n=36, experiment fixed physical layout, no source wind treatment, no plant species interactions, no experimentally randomized cue-revelation order. Freeze results positive or null.
